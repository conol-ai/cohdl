## Summary

The PCF85063A is a low-power CMOS real-time clock and calendar with alarm, countdown timer, offset register and a programmable clock output, on a 400 kHz I²C bus (ds p. 1). It keeps time from 0.9 V to 5.5 V; the I²C interface needs 1.8 V or more (ds p. 34). Supply current is 220 nA typical at 3.3 V and 25 °C with CLKOUT disabled and the bus idle (ds p. 34). It needs an external 32.768 kHz crystal; the load capacitors are integrated and selectable for CL = 7 pF or 12.5 pF (ds p. 1, p. 35). Its I²C address is fixed at 1010 001 (0x51), so only one fits on a bus (ds p. 31). It is specified for −40 °C to +85 °C only (ds p. 2, p. 34). CLKOUT runs at 32.768 kHz from power-on until software disables it (ds p. 14).

## Typical application

- Crystal: 32.768 kHz quartz between OSCI and OSCO, series resistance ≤ 100 kΩ (ds p. 35). The data sheet values assume ESR = 60 kΩ; a 70 kΩ crystal draws a few nA more and starts slightly slower (ds p. 41). Select the load capacitance with CAP_SEL: 0 = 7 pF (default), 1 = 12.5 pF (ds p. 7). No external load capacitors appear in the application diagram (ds p. 41).
- Decoupling: Figure 31 shows 100 nF on VDD (ds p. 41).
- Backup: Figure 31 shows a 1 F super capacitor behind a low-VF diode and an inrush resistor R1 (ds p. 41).
- Pull-ups: SDA, SCL and INT need pull-up resistors (ds p. 41). Tie them to the same supply as the RTC's VDD (ds p. 4, note 1; p. 41, note 2). Size R from tr and Cb (ds p. 41).
- I²C address: 1010 001 binary (0x51 as a 7-bit address); no address pins (ds p. 31).
- CLKOUT: on at power-on at 32.768 kHz. Disable it with COF[2:0] = 111, or on the DFN with CLKOE low; a disabled CLKOUT drives LOW (ds p. 14). Leave CLKOUT disabled when not used: IDD is specified with it disabled (ds p. 34).
- CLKOE (DFN only): an input that must be at a defined level (ds p. 4). Tie it high to allow CLKOUT, low to force it off.
- Power-up: ramp VDD monotonically within tens of microseconds, then set the SR bit (software reset 58h to register 00h) once I²C works (ds p. 10, p. 42). For a power cycle, hold VDD at 0 V for more than 100 ms (ds p. 42). NXP asks for a test point that can ground VDD when a full discharge is not otherwise possible (ds p. 41, note 3).
- Reset state: 00:00:00, 1 January 2000, Saturday; Seconds bit OS = 1 (ds p. 10, p. 11).

## Layout notes

- SO8 reflow footprint (Figure 36, ds p. 48): 0.60 × 1.30 mm lands, 1.27 mm pitch, 4.00 mm between row inner edges, 6.60 mm across outer edges, 5.50 × 7.00 mm occupied area. `SOIC8P127X600X175N` puts 1.3 × 0.6 mm pads at x = ±2.65 mm, which gives the same 4.00 mm and 6.60 mm. It matches.
- TSSOP8 reflow footprint (Figure 38, ds p. 50): 1.150 mm land length, 3.200 mm between row inner edges, 5.500 mm across outer edges. The inner lands (pins 2, 3, 6, 7) are 0.450 mm wide at 0.650 mm pitch. The four corner lands (pins 1, 4, 5, 8) are 0.600 mm wide and 0.725 mm from their neighbours. See "Not established" for the library's difference.
- DFN2626-10 reflow footprint (Figure 37, ds p. 49): P = 0.5, Ay = 3.05, By = 1.9, D = 0.25, SLx = 2.2, SLy = 1.3, SPx = 0.8, SPy = 0.4, Gx = 2.5, Gy = 2.85, Hx = 2.85, Hy = 3.3 mm, 0.025 mm solder-resist clearance. NXP calls it a "generic footprint pattern" and refers to the package outline for the actual layout (ds p. 49). `FP_NXP_DFN2626_10_SOT1197_1` reproduces these values turned 90°: 0.575 × 0.25 mm leads at x = ±1.2375 mm, a 1.3 × 2.2 mm centre land with one 0.4 × 0.8 mm paste opening, and 0.025 mm mask expansion.
- DFN exposed pad: it may be soldered to an electrically isolated copper pad for heat transfer, but this is not required. Never run traces under it (ds p. 5, note 2). Exposed pad Dh 2.10–2.20 mm, Eh 1.20–1.30 mm (ds p. 44).
- Do not bias the part during reflow, for example from a coin cell already fitted; if you do, the supply must allow a full 0 V reset afterwards (ds p. 34, note 1).

## Pitfalls

- Pull-ups to a rail other than the RTC's VDD are against NXP's recommendation (ds p. 4, note 1). The I²C interface is 5 V tolerant (ds p. 35, note 5), but VIH and VIL scale with VDD (ds p. 34, p. 35).
- With a residual VDD at power-up the registers can be corrupted. Either let VDD start from 0 V or send the software reset (ds p. 10, p. 42).
- A slow or non-monotonic VDD ramp can cause a POR failure and a stuck I²C bus. The MCU firmware should implement the bus-clear sequence (ds p. 42).
- Below 1.8 V the clock keeps running but the I²C interface is out of specification (ds p. 34).
- The address 0x51 is fixed (ds p. 31). Two PCF85063A on one bus conflict.
- CLKOUT runs at 32.768 kHz from power-on and costs current until disabled (ds p. 14, p. 34).
- The DFN exposed pad must not be tied to VSS by layout intent; it is high-ohmic to VSS and must stay isolated (ds p. 5, note 2).
- Industrial range only: −40 °C to +85 °C (ds p. 2, p. 34).
- Store and transport the parts at +8 °C to +45 °C and 25 % to 75 % humidity (ds p. 34, note 5); the −65 °C to +150 °C storage rating is a stress limit only.

## Not established by the sources

The library's pin numbers, names and pin-1 locations agree with the source for all three packages (ds pp. 3–4). Disagreements and gaps:

- **TSSOP8 land:** the library comment says it is NXP Figure 38 with "1.15 x 0.45 mm lands, 0.65 mm pitch". Figure 38 makes the corner lands (pins 1, 4, 5, 8) 0.600 mm wide and puts them 0.725 mm from their neighbours, with 2.950 mm across their outer solder-resist edges (ds p. 50). `SOP8P65X490X110N` makes all eight lands 0.45 mm wide on a uniform 0.65 mm pitch, so its corner lands are narrower and about 0.075 mm further in than NXP's. The row spacing and land length match.
- **INT open-drain typing:** the library types INT as `output` and names it `INT`. The datasheet marks it active low (overbar) and open-drain (ds p. 4). An ERC that treats `output` as always driving will not ask for the required pull-up or allow a wired-OR interrupt line.
- **OSCI comment:** the library comment on OSCI says "input must remain at a defined level (Table 3)". Table 3 states that rule for all input pins generally (ds p. 4), but OSCI is the crystal input; the source gives no rule for driving it to a logic level.
- **DFN paste:** the library uses a single 0.4 × 0.8 mm paste opening on the exposed pad. Figure 37 shows SPx × SPy and the symbols nSPx and nSPy but gives no value for the number of openings (ds p. 49).
- **DFN land status:** Figure 37 is labelled a generic footprint pattern (ds p. 49), so it is NXP's recommendation, not a tested land for this part.
- **External clock:** pin CLKOUT becomes an input in EXT_TEST mode (ds p. 7). The library types CLKOUT as `output` only. This matters only for board-level test.
- **Not given:** a guaranteed time-keeping accuracy (it depends on the crystal), the internal pull state of CLKOE, a crystal recommendation by part number, and thermal resistance for any package.
