## Summary

43045-0212 is the 2-circuit, finish-A (reflowed matte tin) part of Molex's Micro-Fit (3.0) vertical through-hole header family (sd p. 1, finish table). The housing is glass-filled LCP, UL94V-0, black, with brass terminals (sd p. 1, note 1). It mates with Micro-Fit (3.0) receptacle series 43025 (sd p. 1, note 5). The two circuits sit in one column on 3.00 mm, with two PCB polarization pegs (sd p. 1). **The sales drawing gives no current or voltage rating.** It refers to product specification PS-43045 (sd p. 1, note 3), which is not in the repository.

## Typical application

- Mates with Micro-Fit (3.0) receptacle series 43025 (sd p. 1, note 5). The drawing names no specific receptacle or crimp terminal. The mating BOM in `lib/connectors/docs/README.md` comes from outside this source.

## Layout notes

- The PCB layout is the component-side view (sd p. 1, "PCB LAYOUT: COMPONENT SIDE"). Holes are Ø1.02 ± 0.05 mm, the same for contact and peg holes (TYP). Rows are 3.00 ± 0.10 mm apart. The peg holes sit 0.94 ± 0.05 mm beyond the row that holds the last circuit, C = 6.00 mm apart and centred on the contact column (sd p. 1).
- In that view, circuit 1 is in the lower row and the last circuit in the upper row, which is the row next to the pegs (sd p. 1).
- **Library check, done by the agent.** `FP_Molex_43045_0212` has pad 1 at (0, 0), pad 2 at (0, 3) and NPTH Ø1.02 at (±3, 3.94), in CoHDL's +y-down top-view frame. Rotating the component-side layout 180° gives exactly this: circuit 2 and the pegs at +y, pegs ±C/2 = ±3.00 mm, 3.00 + 0.94 = 3.94 mm. No reflection is involved (sd p. 1, PCB layout figure).
- The library's 1.50 mm copper ring is not on the drawing. The drawing gives only hole sizes (sd p. 1).
- Molex may ship the part with only one of the two polarization pegs (sd p. 1, note by the 3-D view). Drill both holes.

## Pitfalls

- The pegs mark the last-circuit side. A board that swaps pad 1 and pad 2 relative to the peg holes accepts the header only if it is turned round, and that swaps the circuits.
- Do not take a current rating from catalogues or memory into a design that cites this note. The pinned source has none (sd p. 1, note 3).

## Not established by the sources

- Current rating, voltage rating, temperature range, mating cycles and derating. All of these are in PS-43045, which is not vendored. The figures in `lib/connectors/docs/README.md` ("8.5 A", "600 V", "−40 to +105 °C", "30 cycles") are not backed by a pinned source.
- Copper annulus. The library uses 1.50 mm; the drawing gives none.
- Whether the peg holes should be plated. The library uses NPTH; the drawing does not say.
- How the housing outline sits relative to the holes in plan view. The agent could not dimension it from the drawing, so the library courtyard's y extent (−2.97 to +6.81 mm) was not checked.
- Revision date. `lib/connectors/docs/README.md` gives "Rev G1, 2021-01-11", but that date does not appear on the drawing. The title block shows G1 with EC UCP2016-4586 dated 2015/12/10 and approval on 2016/08/23 (sd p. 1).
