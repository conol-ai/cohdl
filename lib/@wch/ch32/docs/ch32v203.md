# CH32V203C8T6

QingKe V4B RISC-V, up to 144 MHz, 64 KB flash and 20 KB SRAM, LQFP48.
Use `wch_ch32::MCU_CH32V203C8T6`.
Primary evidence: WCH CH32V203DS0 V3.0, Table 2-1, sections 2.5.2/2.5.3/3.1,
Table 3-1-1 and section 5.7; see [sources](sources.md).

## Connections

- VDD_2 pin 36 supplies the main domain. VDD_IO_1 pin 24 and VDD_IO_3 pin 48
  supply the I/O domain. These remain separate required terminals in the
  model. Connect VSS_1/2/3 at pins 23/35/47 and VSSA at pin 8 to ground.
- VDD and VIO operate at 2.4-3.6 V. In normal operation VIO must not exceed
  VDD; VDDA pin 9 must track VIO. A common 3.3 V supply is the straightforward
  arrangement. Provide local bypassing at every supply pin and apply the
  manufacturer's power-sequencing requirements.
- VBAT pin 1 supplies the backup domain (1.8-3.6 V). Connect it deliberately
  even when a separate backup battery is not used. No VCAP or exposed-pad
  terminal exists on this LQFP48 package.
- BOOT0 pin 44 and PB2/BOOT1 pin 20 select boot behavior. Plan the reset-time
  levels and recovery access; the presence of USB does not establish that a
  particular firmware image or bootloader mode is ready to use.
- PA13 pin 34 and PA14 pin 37 provide WCH's two-wire debug interface. Although
  the pins are named SWDIO/SWCLK in WCH's table, use a compatible WCH probe
  and firmware/toolchain. NRST is a dedicated input at pin 7.
- PD0/PD1 pins 5/6 default to OSC_IN/OSC_OUT and require remapping for GPIO.
  Low-frequency crystal pins are PC14/PC15 at pins 3/4.
- USB D-/D+ use PA11/PA12 at pins 32/33; the other USBFS pair uses PB6/PB7
  at pins 42/43, sharing I2C1 functions. CAN1 RX/TX also multiplex PA11/PA12.
  Verify resource availability, routing and clock requirements for this exact
  C8 part. A CAN bus connection requires an external transceiver.

## Land pattern

`qfp::KICAD_LQFP_48_7X7MM_P0_5MM` from `qfp 0.3.0` matches section 5.7's
nominal 7 x 7 mm body, 9 x 9 mm lead span, 0.5 mm pitch and 48 terminals.
The source package drawing is rotated as a whole, without mirroring, onto the
assembler's frame (library plan W3): pin 1 at the left end of the lower row;
numbering remains counterclockwise in component-side view.

The generic rounded rectangular pads are 1.475 x 0.3 mm at row centers
+/-4.1625 mm (vertical equivalents on top/bottom), with 0.2 mm adjacent
copper gap and 10.3 x 10.3 mm courtyard. Nominal 0.6 mm lead feet occupy
radii 3.9-4.5 mm, within the copper's radial extent 3.425-4.9 mm.
This is nominal dimensional compatibility, not a WCH-recommended land or a
full tolerance-stack validation. WCH's broad tolerance note in section 5
requires further package and assembly qualification, as explained in the
package [README](README.md). KiCad provenance remains in the dependency.
