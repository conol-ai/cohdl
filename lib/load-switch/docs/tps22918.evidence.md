## Application boundaries

**QOD is not reverse-current blocking.** Datasheet section 9.2.2.2 (p17)
identifies body-diode current from VOUT into VIN when the output remains
charged after input power is removed. This part alone does not establish a
backfeed barrier.

Sections 8.3.2–8.3.2.2 (pp13–14) describe discharge into ground, whose strength
weakens as VIN falls. QOD may float, connect directly to VOUT, or connect
through a resistor. An externally driven output must not feed the internal
discharge path.

[TI's support clarification](https://e2e.ti.com/support/power-management-group/power-management/f/power-management-forum/767428/tps22918-discharge-timing)
says QOD cannot be guaranteed when VIN rapidly falls below 1 V. Treat this
as support guidance, not an additional electrical specification.
