# SGM2554A — SGMICRO 1.1A power distribution switch with enable, SOT-23-5

`LOADSW_SGM2554` is the A model, ordering code `SGM2554AYN5G/TR` (SOT-23-5,
tape and reel 3000; datasheet p. 2).

Pinout (SGM2554A, SOT-23-5, top view; datasheet p. 3):
1=VOUT, 2=GND, 3=VIN, 4=EN, 5=VOUT. Pins 1 and 5 are both the switch
output; the device declares them as one pin, `VOUT: 1, 5`.

- Input voltage 2.2-5.5V (p. 1, p. 4); input absolute maximum 6V (p. 2).
  Continuous current 1.1A (p. 1; the source states no package or PCB
  condition for it).
- EN: chip enable, logic high enables the device (p. 3). VIH >= 1.5V,
  VIL <= 0.4V at 25C (p. 4); EN absolute range -0.3V to 6V (p. 2). The
  datasheet does not say whether EN has an internal pull or what a floating
  EN does, so drive it or bias it externally.
- SGM2554B (`SGM2554BYN5G/TR`) is a different pinout: it has no enable and
  pin 4 is a second VIN (p. 3). It is not modelled here and is not a
  substitute for this part.
- Typical application (p. 1): 1uF from VIN to GND and 1uF from VOUT to GND,
  placed at the pins.
- Used in the three-controller watch design as U9 to gate the ESP32-S3
  VDD3P3 rail; EN driven by SF32 GPIO PA11.

Land (`FP_SGM2554_SOT235`): the datasheet's recommended land pattern
(p. 11), 0.69 x 0.99mm pads, 0.95mm pitch, 1.90mm between the outer pads
of a row, 2.59mm between pad-row centres. The courtyard (4.1 x 3.6mm)
contains the pads and the maximum body length D = 3.050mm (p. 11).

Datasheet: SGMICRO SGM2554 Power Distribution Switch, April 2022,
REV. A.4 (www.sg-micro.com), committed locally as `sgm2554.pdf` (SHA-256
58d6e47c130c08cdf6f49f1b7844eb9079b0255018a846df5ec0516e2c002a87).

Before load-switch 0.2.0 this device declared 1=IN, 2=GND, 3=EN, 4=NC,
5=OUT, a 1.0-5.5V input, the MPN `SGM2554`, and 0.60 x 1.00mm pads on
2.60mm rows, and claimed the land matched an RT9080 footprint in
`lib/@contrib/ldo`. None of that is in the source; see the package README.
