## Summary

The RS0104 is a 4-bit non-inverting, auto-direction level translator. The A port runs from 1.65 V to 5.5 V and the B port from 2.3 V to 5.5 V, with VCCA ≤ VCCB (ds p. 1, p. 9). It is a pass-gate design with one-shot edge accelerators and an internal 10 kΩ pull-up on every I/O, aimed at open-drain buses such as I²C or 1-Wire, with push-pull use also possible (ds p. 22, p. 24). Typical data rates are 20–24 Mbit/s push-pull and 2 Mbit/s open-drain (ds p. 12). OE is active high with an internal pull-down; OE low puts all I/Os in high-Z and turns off the pull-ups (ds p. 1, p. 4, p. 23). Do not use it where VCCA must exceed VCCB, where a DC load needs more than the 10 kΩ pull-up can source, or on long, heavily loaded lines that can outlast the roughly 30 ns one-shot (ds p. 9, p. 22, p. 23). All switching figures are typical only (ds pp. 12–16).

## Typical application

- Supplies: 1 µF and 0.1 µF on each of VCCA and VCCB in the typical application (ds p. 24). Keep VCCA ≤ VCCB (ds p. 9). Either supply may ramp first (ds p. 1).
- OE: the typical application drives it from the A-side controller (ds p. 24). It is referenced to VCCA and needs VIH ≥ 0.8 × VCCA (ds p. 4, p. 9). To keep the I/Os high-Z during power-up and power-down, tie OE to GND through a pull-down resistor whose minimum value is set by the driver's source current (ds p. 1).
- Pull-ups: none needed for open-drain buses; each I/O has 10 kΩ to its port supply. Add external pull-ups in parallel only if a lower value is needed, at the cost of a higher VOL (ds p. 23).
- Use it point to point; its main target is open-drain I/O such as I²C and 1-Wire (ds p. 24).
- NC pins (TSSOP-14 and QFN14 pads 6 and 9) have no internal connection (ds p. 4, p. 7).

## Layout notes

- TSSOP-14 recommended land (ds p. 26): 0.42 × 1.78 mm pads, 0.65 mm pitch, 5.6 mm between row centres. `soic::SOP14P65X640X120N` uses 1.78 × 0.42 mm pads at x = ±2.8 mm and 0.65 mm pitch. It matches.
- QFN lands: the source gives package dimensions only, no land pattern, for QFN3.5x3.5-14L (ds p. 25), QFN2x2-12L (ds p. 27) and QFN2x1.7-12L (ds p. 28). The library's QFN lands are its own derivations.
- Keep data traces short so that the round-trip delay of a reflection is shorter than the one-shot, and minimise lumped load capacitance (ds p. 23).
- QFN exposed pads: solder to the PCB and connect to GND, or leave floating (ds p. 4, p. 5).

## Pitfalls

- VCCA must not exceed VCCB (ds p. 9). Swapping the two ports between a 3.3 V and a 1.8 V system is outside the specification; the lower supply goes on A.
- VIL on the data I/Os is only 0.15 V max (ds p. 9). Drivers with a weak low or a series resistor may not reach it.
- The I/Os source only hundreds of microamps through the internal 10 kΩ pull-ups (ds p. 22). Do not use the part to drive a DC load high.
- The internal pull-ups turn off when OE is low (ds p. 23). An I²C bus that relies on them floats while the translator is disabled.
- OE's internal pull-down works only while VCCA is powered (ds p. 1).
- Pad maps differ between packages: pad 1 is VCCA on TSSOP-14, QFN14 and QFN2x1.7, but A1 on QFN2x2-12L (ds pp. 4–7). Use the footprint bound to each part.
- All timing and data-rate figures are typical (ds pp. 12–16); there are no guaranteed maximums.

## Not established by the sources

- **@contrib/level-shifter ≤0.1.x: QFN3.5x3.5-14L land, pads 1, 7, 8 and 14 (serious); fixed in 0.2.0 (PR #95).** In ≤0.1.x, `QFN14N50P350X350_1EP200X150` puts pads 1 and 14 at x = ∓0.25 mm on the top edge and pads 7 and 8 at x = ∓0.25 mm on the bottom edge, 0.5 mm apart. The package drawing dimensions those two-lead edges with E1 = 1.500 mm TYP between the centres of leads 1 and 14 (and of 7 and 8) (ds p. 25, bottom view). The leads sit about ±0.75 mm from centre. With 0.4 mm wide lands at ±0.25 mm and 0.18–0.30 mm wide leads at ±0.75 mm, the lands and leads do not overlap. VCCA, VCCB, GND and OE would be open on a board built with the ≤0.1.x footprint. 0.2.0 moves pads 1/14 and 7/8 to x = ∓0.75 mm and leaves the other ten lands, the pad sizes and the footprint name unchanged. The ≤0.1.x library comment reads D1 = 2.000 and E1 = 1.500 as the exposed-pad size. On ds p. 25, D1 is the centre span of leads 2–6 (4 × 0.5 mm) and E1 the lead 1-to-14 spacing. The exposed-pad size is not dimensioned at all, so the library's 2.0 × 1.5 mm EP land is not supported by the source. 0.2.0 keeps that land and its comment calls it a library choice, not a manufacturer value. `lcsc_map.json` maps RS0104YTQF14 to C5372421.
- **QFN2x1.7-12L land clearance:** the library's end lands (pads 6 and 12, x = ±0.1 mm) and the nearest side lands (pads 1, 5, 7 and 11, inner edge x = ±0.15 mm) are 0.05 mm apart. The leads themselves end at least about 0.2 mm from centre (E ≥ 1.6, L ≤ 0.6 mm), and the end leads are b1 = 0.15 mm REF wide (ds p. 28). The source gives no end-lead length, so the end-land length is the library's assumption. `examples/sf32-miniboard` uses this part (`LS_RS0104_QFN12_2X1P7`). No example uses the QFN14 part.
- **QFN2x2-12L land clearance:** the library's exposed-pad land uses the maximum D2/E2 = 1.2 mm (ds p. 27). It is 0.075 mm from the lead lands. The source gives no land pattern to compare with.
- **README:** `docs/README.md` says the datasheet has "not manufacturer PCB-land recommendations". The TSSOP-14 page has one (ds p. 26), and the shared soic land follows it. The statement holds for the three QFN packages.
- **Datasheet errors:** the QFN3.5x3.5 E row gives inch values 0.007–0.012, copied from b (ds p. 25). The application text says "the RS0104 might be a better option for such push-pull applications", referring to itself (ds p. 24). VOHA has a 5.5 V maximum but VOHB has none (ds p. 11). The QFN2x2-12L pin table's type footnote is numbered (2), not (1) (ds p. 5).
- **Not given:** maximum (guaranteed) propagation delays and data rates, the internal pull-up tolerance, the OE pull-down current, thermal resistance for any package, and the exposed-pad size of QFN3.5x3.5-14L.
