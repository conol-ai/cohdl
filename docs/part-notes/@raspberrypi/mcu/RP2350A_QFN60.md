## Summary

RP2350A is Raspberry Pi's dual-core microcontroller: two Cortex-M33 or two Hazard3 RISC-V cores at 150 MHz, 520 kB SRAM, 8 kB OTP, USB 1.1 host/device with on-chip PHY, 12 PIO state machines and HSTX (ds p. 14). The A suffix is the 7 × 7 mm QFN-60 with 30 GPIOs, 4 of them ADC inputs, and no flash in the package; program code lives in an external QSPI flash of up to 16 MB, with a second 16 MB device possible on a second chip select (ds p. 14). The core runs from 1.1 V made by an on-chip 200 mA switching regulator that needs an external 3.3 µH polarity-marked inductor and a layout copied closely from Raspberry Pi's (ds p. 449, ds p. 453, ds p. 456, hw p. 8). Choose it when you want a cheap, well-documented MCU with PIO and USB boot; do not choose it if you cannot follow the regulator layout rules, or if you need more than 30 GPIOs (RP2350B, QFN-80) or flash in the package (RP2354A) (ds p. 14).

## Typical application

- **Supplies.** A single 3.3 V supply is typical: IOVDD, QSPI_IOVDD, USB_OTP_VDD, ADC_AVDD, VREG_VIN and VREG_AVDD on 3.3 V, DVDD from the on-chip regulator (ds p. 442, hw p. 6). VREG_VIN and VREG_AVDD can share one supply only if it is within 3.135–3.63 V (Figure 19); above that, power them separately (Figure 20) (ds p. 451, ds p. 452). USB_OTP_VDD must always be powered, even if USB is never used, and VREG_AVDD must always be powered because it also feeds power-on reset and brown-out detection (ds p. 443). Power VREG_VIN and VREG_AVDD up together; other rails may sequence freely (ds p. 444).
- **Core regulator parts** (ds p. 456, hw p. 6, hw p. 8):
  - CIN on VREG_VIN: at least 4.7 µF, at most 50 mΩ parasitic resistance (hw: C6, 4.7 µF 0402 6.3 V X5R).
  - COUT on the 1.1 V output after the inductor: 4.7 µF ±20%, at most 250 mΩ and 6 nH (hw: C7, 4.7 µF 0402 6.3 V X5R).
  - L between VREG_LX and the 1.1 V net: fully shielded, 3.3 µH ±20%, DCR at most 250 mΩ, saturation current at least 1.5 A, marked for polarity. Raspberry Pi recommends Abracon AOTA-B201610S3R3-101-T, 2.0 × 1.6 mm (ds p. 456, ds p. 457, ds p. 458).
  - VREG_AVDD: RC filter, 33 Ω from 3.3 V and 4.7 µF to ground (hw: R3, C9) (hw p. 6, hw p. 8, ds p. 452).
  - VREG_FB is fed from the output of COUT; the 1.1 V net also goes to all three DVDD pins (ds p. 456, hw p. 6).
- **External 1.1 V instead** (Figure 21): omit the inductor, connect VREG_FB to ground, keep VREG_VIN and VREG_AVDD powered with one input capacitor, and write the VREG HIZ bit; until then the regulator draws about 400 µA (ds p. 452, ds p. 453). Never use the regulator's low-power mode with an external DVDD (ds p. 449).
- **Decoupling.** 100 nF per power pin is the general rule (hw p. 9). The datasheet asks for 100 nF on the two DVDD pins nearest the regulator and 4.7 µF on the DVDD pin furthest from it (ds p. 442), and recommends a second 4.7 µF on the 1.1 V net at DVDD pin 23, away from LX/COUT (ds p. 456). USB_OTP_VDD and ADC_AVDD each get 100 nF; VREG_VIN gets 4.7 µF (ds p. 443). The minimal design uses 100 nF on each DVDD pin and seven 100 nF on the 3.3 V pins, with pins 53 and 54 sharing one capacitor (C12) for space (hw p. 9).
- **Flash and BOOTSEL.** Wire QSPI_SS/SCLK/SD0–SD3 directly to a QSPI flash with short traces; the reference uses W25Q128JVSIQ with 100 nF on VCC (hw p. 11). The bootrom probes with 03h serial read at about 1 MHz and programs with 02h, 05h, 06h and 20h (hw p. 12, hw p. 13). QSPI_SS is sampled shortly after reset: low enters BOOTSEL. Pull it low only through a resistor: hw uses 1 kΩ (R6) to the BOOTSEL button; ds calls 4.7 kΩ "a good intermediate value" (hw p. 11, ds p. 375, ds p. 376). A 10 kΩ pull-up from QSPI_SS to 3.3 V (R1) is DNF for W25Q128JV but may be needed with other flash (hw p. 11).
- **UART boot.** BOOTSEL with QSPI_SD1 driven high selects UART boot on QSPI_SD2 (TX) and QSPI_SD3 (RX), 1 Mbaud 8n1 (ds p. 376, ds p. 417).
- **Crystal.** Abracon ABM8-272-T3, 12 MHz, CL = 10 pF, ESR ≤ 50 Ω, ±30 ppm, drive level 200 µW max (ds p. 556, hw p. 14). Circuit: XIN to one crystal terminal, XOUT through 1 kΩ (R2) to the other, 15 pF C0G from each terminal to ground, crystal case pads grounded (hw p. 14). 15 pF / 2 + 3 pF assumed parasitics = 10.5 pF (hw p. 14). The circuit is tuned for IOVDD = 3.3 V; any other crystal or IOVDD needs retuning and testing (hw p. 14, ds p. 557).
- **RUN.** Active-low chip enable/reset with an internal pull-up at reset (ds p. 1338). "If RUN is not used, it should be tied high" (ds p. 498). The minimal design has a reset button SW2 from RUN to ground through 1 kΩ (R4) and no external pull-up (hw p. 21). Double-tapping RUN can select an alternate boot (ds p. 498).
- **USB.** USB_DP/USB_DM need 27 Ω series resistors close to the chip; the pull-ups and pull-downs are internal, so add none (ds p. 1336, hw p. 16).
- **SWD.** SWCLK and SWDIO have internal pull-ups at reset (ds p. 1338). Connect probe ground directly as well (ds p. 1343).
- **ADC pins.** For analog use set IE = 0 and OD = 1 in the pad register (ds p. 1069).

## Layout notes

- Place and route the core regulator first and copy Figures 22–23 (Pico 2) as closely as possible (ds p. 453, ds p. 455). Keep the CIN/L/COUT switching loops small, all on the same side as the chip, and join their high-current ground to the QFN ground pad at one point with two adjacent vias (ds p. 455, ds p. 456).
- Give CFILT (the VREG_AVDD capacitor) its own low-impedance ground path to the QFN pad, not shared with CIN/COUT (ds p. 455, ds p. 456).
- Cut copper away under the inductor and around VREG_LX; on 4+ layer boards also under L/VREG_LX on layer 2 (Figure 24) (ds p. 455, ds p. 456).
- Orient the inductor as Raspberry Pi shows: field direction matters even for shielded parts (ds p. 457, hw p. 7).
- The Pico 2 bottom layer under the regulator is a ground plane joined to the QFN GND pad (ds p. 455).
- Land pattern, Figure 144: 60 lands 0.875 × 0.20 mm at 0.40 mm pitch, 7.75 mm outer and 6.00 mm inner span, 3.40 × 3.40 mm centre pad (ds p. 1329). `qfn::QFN60N40P700X700_1EP340X340` matches this: lands centred at ±3.4375 mm, ±2.80 mm along each edge, pad 1 at top left, numbering anticlockwise, one 3.4 mm square pad 61.
- USB: aim for 90 Ω differential over unbroken ground; the guide's 0.8 mm tracks with 0.15 mm gap apply only to its 1 mm two-layer board (hw p. 16).
- Keep crystal traces short; their capacitance is part of the load (hw p. 14). Keep QSPI traces short and put the QSPI_SS resistors next to the flash (hw p. 11, hw p. 12).

## Pitfalls

- **Table 1432's IOVDD row is wrong.** It lists IOVDD as 11, 20, 30, 38, 45, 54 (ds p. 1339). Pin 54 is QSPI_IOVDD and pin 1 is IOVDD in Figure 148 (ds p. 1335) and in the minimal-design symbol (hw p. 21). The library follows the figure.
- Do not take QFN-80 (RP2350B) pin numbers from Figure 149 or the second column of Tables 1427–1432 (ds p. 1335, ds p. 1336).
- GPIO26–GPIO29 (pins 40–43) are not fault-tolerant: their inputs must stay at or below IOVDD, even when ADC_AVDD is higher (ds p. 1337, ds p. 443, ds p. 1069). The other GPIOs tolerate 5.5 V only while IOVDD is powered at 3.3 V (ds p. 1336, ds p. 1340).
- With IOVDD at 1.8 V, set VOLTAGE_SELECT. Running IO above 1.8 V with the 1.8 V thresholds selected may damage the chip (ds p. 442).
- Without a crystal or an XIN clock there is no USB and no BOOTSEL: both USB and UART boot need XOSC (ds p. 376, ds p. 1343, hw p. 14).
- The 1.1 V DVDD net is the inductor's output, not VREG_LX itself (Figure 19, ds p. 452; Figure 3, hw p. 6). Software can set the regulator output above DVDD's limit (ds p. 1346).
- Pulling QSPI_SS hard to ground breaks flash access after boot; keep the series resistor (hw p. 11).
- Silicon stepping is not in the MPN: markings carry A2, A3 or A4 (ds p. 1332). Erratum RP2350-E9 (A2 only, fixed in A3) makes a Bank 0 input source about 120 µA at mid-rail when IE is on; a pull of 8.2 kΩ or less, or IE = 0, avoids it (ds p. 1367, ds p. 1368, ds p. 1369).
- The Table 1446 currents are typical examples, not limits (ds p. 1348).
- Sum of all GPIO source current is 100 mA, and of QSPI pins 20 mA (ds p. 1341).

## Not established by the sources

Library and package-doc claims compared with the sources, most important first:

- **`RUN` is declared `optional`; in @raspberrypi/mcu ≤0.1.4 its comment says SWCLK, SWDIO and RUN "need not be routed in every design" (comment corrected in 0.1.5, PR #99).** The datasheet says "If RUN is not used, it should be tied high" (Section 7.3.3, ds p. 498). RUN does have an internal pull-up at reset (Table 1430, ds p. 1338), but the source does not say it may float. Leaving RUN unconnected is not established. 0.1.5 keeps RUN `optional` but its comment and application note say to tie it high or drive it from a reset circuit.
- **`XIN`/`XOUT` are `optional`** (≤0.1.4 comment: "Optional 12 MHz crystal oscillator"). The guide agrees an external clock is not strictly required (hw p. 14), but BOOTSEL mode (USB and UART boot) requires a crystal or an XIN clock (Section 5.2.8.1, ds p. 376). A board without one cannot use the USB bootloader. In 0.1.5 (PR #99) the pins stay `optional` and the comment states this BOOTSEL requirement.
- **Pin types the library cannot express.**
  - `QSPI_SS` is typed `output`, but the bootrom samples it as the BOOTSEL input (ds p. 375). The type is unchanged in 0.1.5; its comment there records the BOOTSEL role.
  - `VREG_PGND` and `GND` are typed `power_in`; both are grounds (ds p. 1339).
  - `VREG_FB` is `passive`; the datasheet calls it the regulator feedback input (ds p. 1339).
  - `DVDD` is `power_in` with no `power_out` on its net: the 1.1 V comes from `VREG_LX` (`passive`) through the inductor (ds p. 452).
- **The footprint's paste and vias.** Figure 144 gives copper only (ds p. 1329); no paste segmentation, thermal-via array or courtyard is specified. The library's single 3.4 mm paste opening and 8.25 mm courtyard are not from the source.
- **Absolute maximum ratings are incomplete.** Table 1433 lists DVDD, IOVDD/QSPI_IOVDD and pin voltages only; VREG_VIN, VREG_AVDD, USB_OTP_VDD and ADC_AVDD have no absolute maximum (ds p. 1339, ds p. 1340). No maximum operating junction temperature is given beyond the -40 to 125 °C absolute rating.
- **Datasheet and guide disagree on DVDD decoupling.** The datasheet wants 4.7 µF on the DVDD pin furthest from the regulator (ds p. 442) and recommends an extra 4.7 µF at pin 23 (ds p. 456). The minimal design fits only 100 nF on each DVDD pin plus COUT (hw p. 9, hw p. 21). In @raspberrypi/mcu ≤0.1.4, `rp2350-application.md` says "Place 100 nF close to each supply pin, including each DVDD pin", then mentions the extra 4.7 µF; it does not quote the p. 442 rule. 0.1.5 (PR #99) quotes it and notes that the two sources are not reconciled.
- **Datasheet text inconsistency.** Section 6.3.8.1 says COUT "must be placed between VREG_VIN and VREG_PGND" (ds p. 456), while Figures 19 and 22 put the output capacitor on the 1.1 V net (ds p. 452). The sentence probably means CIN; the source does not say.
- **The guide's crystal table is missing.** Section 4.1 ends "has the following specifications:" with no table (hw p. 15); the specification is Table 597 in the datasheet (ds p. 556).
- **Load capacitance.** 10.5 pF from 15 pF caps rests on an assumed 3 pF of parasitics (hw p. 14); the XIN/XOUT pin capacitance is not specified.
- **Power tables name a rail `ADC_IOVDD`** (Tables 1445, 1446, ds p. 1348); the pin is `ADC_AVDD` everywhere else.
- **Package docs checked and found consistent (as of @raspberrypi/mcu 0.1.4):** `docs/README.md` (Figure 144 at PDF pp. 1328–1329; Figure 148; the Table 1432 error) and `docs/rp2350-application.md` (pin numbers, Table 1441 ranges, regulator component values at p. 456, crystal and USB circuits at hw pp. 14 and 16, the Table 1446 sum of 15.962 mA and the RP2350-E9 summary) agree with the pages cited. The library pin map matches Figure 148 pad for pad.
- Not given by these sources: a recommended LDO or input supply, ESD protection for USB, a land-pattern solder-mask rule, and any qualification of inductors other than the Abracon part (ds p. 457).
