## Notes from the sheets

- PSM712-LF-T7 (C32677): Package outline page note 'Pin 3 is the cathode (Unidirectional Only)' is a generic package note, not applicable. Polarity is the OPPOSITE of the Hongjiacheng SM712 (C7420375) datasheet.
- H12VNT2B (C20617927): Datasheet title 'Bi-directional'; array of 2 lines to common pin 3.
- H15VNT2B (C20617928): Datasheet title 'Bi-directional'; array of 2 lines to common pin 3.
- H24VNT2B (C20615827): Datasheet title 'Bi-directional'; array of 2 lines to common pin 3.
- H36VLT2B (C20615828): Datasheet title 'Bi-directional'; array of 2 lines to common pin 3.
- H3V3HT2B (C20617923): Datasheet title 'Bi-directional'; array of 2 lines to common pin 3.
- H5VHT2B (C20617926): Datasheet title 'Bi-directional'; array of 2 lines to common pin 3.
- H5VNT2B (C20617925): Datasheet title 'Bi-directional'; array of 2 lines to common pin 3. Marking L5A breaks the xB2 pattern of the sibling bidirectional parts.
- H8VNT2B (C20615825): Datasheet title 'Bi-directional'; array of 2 lines to common pin 3.
- H5VUT2U (C20615824): Titled 'Uni-directional 5V Ultra Small Capacitance ESD' but internally a rail-clamp steering-diode array, not a plain zener pair; differs from the H..T2U siblings.
- H12VNT2U (C20615826): Datasheet title 'Uni-directional'; array of 2 lines, common anode pin 3.
- H3V3HT2U (C20617924): Datasheet title 'Uni-directional'; array of 2 lines, common anode pin 3.
- H5VHT2U (C20615823): Datasheet title 'Uni-directional'; array of 2 lines, common anode pin 3. Datasheet inconsistency: VC max 7.0 V @ 1 A is below VBR max 9.0 V.

## Left out

- SM712 (C7420375, Zhuhai Hongjiacheng): its sheet draws the 7 V and 12 V directions opposite to ProTek's PSM712, and its table headers read either way. It waits for the direction to be settled.
