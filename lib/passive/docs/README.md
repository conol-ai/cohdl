# Official source documents

This directory contains manufacturer documents and repository-authored evidence
records used to generate and audit the `passive` package. Evidence records retain
the upstream URL and PDF SHA-256 without redistributing that third-party file.
The catalog is generated by
`tools/gen_passive.py`; source coverage is recorded at series level so 8,972
parts and 8,454 alternates remain reproducible rather than relying on
thousands of hand-edited declarations.

## Footprint audit

`CHIP_0201` through `CHIP_2512` are the nominal
`KiCad/Resistor_SMD.pretty/R_<EIA>_<metric>Metric` reflow lands established by
RFC-021 for both resistors and MLCCs. They were checked dimension by dimension
against:

- Vishay document 20052 page 2 for CRCW0201;
- Vishay document 20035 page 11 for CRCW0402 through CRCW2512; and
- Samsung's catalog page 48 for the metric-equivalent MLCC packages.

The sources intentionally do not prescribe identical lands: the shipped
patterns retain KiCad's general-purpose nominal geometry between the
manufacturer recommendations instead of claiming to reproduce either vendor's
process-specific optimum. Pad count is two, numbering is non-polar, every
pattern is referenced by real parts, and the unused `CHIP_01005` placeholder
was removed.

`FP_L_0806_2016Metric` is the IPC-7351 nominal land for the official
DFE201612E 2.0 x 1.6 mm body and terminal drawing. The new
`FP_L_LQH2MCN_2016Metric` transcribes section 11.1 of `JELF243A-0060P`
directly: 2.6 mm outer copper span, 0.8 mm inner gap, and 1.0 mm pad width,
giving 0.9 x 1.0 mm pads at x = ±0.85 mm. Both have exactly two numbered pads
and are exercised by their corresponding parts.

`FP_L_WPN201610U_2016Metric` directly transcribes Sunlord's page-164
recommended land: 0.7 mm inner gap, two 0.7 x 1.7 mm pads, and pad centres at
x = ±0.7 mm. Page 166 supplies the exact 4.7 uH ordering code and ratings.

Relevant manufacturer pages and package drawings were rendered and visually checked
on 2026-07-30 and 2026-08-05. Temporary render artifacts are not shipped.

`FP_Murata_BLM15PD_1005Metric` transcribes the manufacturer recommendation
for BLM15P parts at up to 1.5 A: 0.4 mm inner gap, nominal 1.3 mm outer span,
and 0.5 x 0.5 mm lands. It is exercised by the exact BLM15PD121SN1D part.

`FP_PANASONIC_OSCON_C6` transcribes Panasonic's C6 OS-CON recommendation:
2.1 mm inner gap, 9.1 mm total copper span and 1.6 mm land width, producing
two 3.5 x 1.6 mm pads centred at x = ±2.8 mm. Pad 2 carries the negative
polarity mark.

`FP_COILCRAFT_0402DC` transcribes Coilcraft Document 1153 directly: two
0.36 x 0.66 mm lands separated by a 0.61 mm inner gap, with centres at
x = ±0.485 mm. The exact 2.0 nH and 3.0 nH parts both exercise this footprint.

## LCSC bindings (passive 0.2.4)

33 entries carry a `supplier lcsc` binding: 25 resistors and 8 MLCCs, from 0201 to 2512 (31 Yageo primaries and two Vishay CRCW alternates). Each part number was placed on the assembler's preview of 2026-11-05 (`tools/sourcing_data/jlc_previews/2026-11-05-c2-board-3/`) and read along its land, so each binding has a zero map. A chip part is not polarised, so its frame is known mod 180 only; that makes no difference to the copper. `tools/gen_passive.py` writes the clauses from `tools/sourcing_data/lcsc_map.json` and `jlc_frames.json`, and an entry without both a confirmed identity and a frame row for this version gets none.

A binding changes no connection, no geometry and no verdict, and no land changed from 0.2.3. A plain `build` writes the same neutral BOM and CPL. The KiCad netlist gains an `LCSC` field for each instance whose primary entry is bound (31 of the 33; the two Vishay bindings are on alternates). `cohdl build DIR --supplier lcsc` adds the LCSC BOM, map and CPL, which carry these part numbers. There an instance takes its bound primary, else its first bound alternate: `R_1K58_F_0402` takes the Vishay C3009257, while `R_820R_J_0402` takes its Yageo primary C137837, so its Vishay C482239 is not selected.

## Stocked Samsung alternates (passive 0.2.6)

0.2.6 adds 37 Samsung CL alternates to existing MLCC parts: 9 on 0402 parts, 22 on 0603 and 6 on 0805. Each is a part number JLCPCB stocks as Basic or Preferred in the B1 snapshot (`tools/sourcing_data/jlc_catalog/2026-10-30/`).
- **How they were chosen.** `tools/samsung_b1_alternates.py` decodes every Samsung CL number in that snapshot with the part-numbering rules of `samsung_mlcc_catalog_2025-12.pdf` (pp. 6–7), and writes `tools/passive_data/samsung_b1.json`. That table holds 39 numbers: 32 Basic, 7 Preferred. A number is kept only when it decodes to a part this package emits, with the tolerance the package pairs with its dielectric: C0G ±5%, X7R and X5R ±10%. Two of the 39 were already alternates: `CL05B104KO5NNNC` (C1525) and `CL10A106KP8NNNC` (C19702).
- **How they are added.** `gen_passive.py` appends each one after the part's existing alternates. Most of these parts already carry a Samsung alternate from `samsung_cl.json`, so the stocked number is a second Samsung entry for the same combination.
- **The 28 not added.** The table lists them with the reason:
  - 10 match a combination whose Yageo primary Yageo will not confirm, so the package emits no part;
  - 9 have ±20% tolerance;
  - 8 decode to a combination the package does not offer;
  - 1 has a rated-voltage code this package has no voltage for.

  (Corrected in 0.2.7. 0.2.6 counted 16 and 3, because the tolerance check ran after the Yageo one: six ±20% numbers were listed under the Yageo reason, as Tony noted on #83.)
- **One new binding.** `C_10u_25V_X5R_0805` binds its new alternate `CL21A106KAYNNNE` to C15850, with a zero map. That part number already had a frame row (`c2b1-c0805-c15850`, C2 frame board 1). That makes 37 bindings.
- **What else changed.** Nothing: no land and no part. The other new alternates have no frame evidence yet, so they are not bound. Their identities are in `lcsc_map.json` (12,321 rows), from the run `lcsc_backfill/2026-11-07-samsung-b1/`. Every `passive` frame row now names 0.2.6.

## Sampled bindings: 0402 Yageo RC (passive 0.2.7)

0.2.7 binds 842 more Yageo `RC` 0402 resistors, for 879 bindings in all. Two kinds of evidence carry them, and they are listed apart:

- **Previewed: 44.** Each is a W3 pilot sample with its own frame row from the assembler's preview of 2026-11-07 (`tools/sourcing_data/jlc_previews/2026-11-07-w3-pilot-0402/`). The rows are those archived in that record's `frames.json`, unchanged apart from the version.
- **Inferred: 798.** No part number among these was previewed itself. Each is inferred under the W3 rule (`docs/library-supplier-w3-sampling.md`, accepted for chip R/C on 2026-11-09) from the group record `tools/sourcing_data/w3_records/2026-11-10-chip0402-yageo-rc.json`. Its frame row cites that record through `sampled`.

**The group and its samples.** The group is `CHIP_0402` / `ChipResistor` / Yageo / `RC`, with 969 members. Its record lists 49 samples, all with a zero map within the rule's sample limits: the 44 pilot samples and 5 rows from C2 frame board 3 already bound since 0.2.4.

**Members with no stock.** The 122 members with no stock on the confirmed pages (`lcsc_backfill/2026-11-07-samsung-b1/confirmed.json`) are not bound. A member gets a binding once it has stock and is inferred from a later record, or once it is previewed itself.

**Lands.** No land changed: `pads.cohdl` and `footprints.cohdl` are byte for byte those of 0.2.6, and the record pins their SHA-256. Editing either file lapses every inferred row in CI. Every `passive` frame row now names 0.2.7.

**What changes in a build.** A plain `build` writes the same neutral BOM and CPL. The KiCad netlist gains an `LCSC` field for each instance whose primary entry is bound. `cohdl build DIR --supplier lcsc` selects these part numbers as for any other binding.
