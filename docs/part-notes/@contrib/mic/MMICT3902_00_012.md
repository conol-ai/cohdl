## Summary

T3902 is a bottom-port omnidirectional MEMS microphone with a 1-bit PDM output from a fourth-order Σ-Δ modulator (ds p. 1, p. 4). The clock frequency selects the mode: sleep below 200 kHz, low-power 400–800 kHz, standard 1.0–3.3 MHz, high-performance 4.1–4.8 MHz (ds p. 6). Sensitivity is −26 dBFS (low-power and standard) or −32 dBFS (high-performance) at 94 dB SPL, with up to 126 dB SPL acoustic overload point in high-performance mode (ds p. 1, p. 4–5). SELECT puts the output on the left or right half of the clock, so two microphones can share one DATA line (ds p. 10, p. 12). It needs a PDM input (codec or MCU); it has no I²S output.

## Typical application

- VDD: 0.1 µF (X7R) to GND, as close to VDD as the PCB allows (ds p. 10, p. 15).
- Mono: SELECT to GND gives the right channel (data on the rising clock edge); tie SELECT to VDD for left (ds p. 12, p. 14, Figure 11).
- Stereo: two microphones share CLK and DATA, one with SELECT = GND and one with SELECT = VDD (Figure 12, ds p. 15). When SELECT is high, tie it to the same supply as that microphone's VDD (ds p. 10).
- No pull-up or pull-down on DATA: it tri-states every half cycle (ds p. 15).
- Long DATA lines (> 15 cm) may need a buffer, placed only after the two microphones are joined; a source-termination resistor on the codec clock output can replace a CLK buffer (ds p. 15–16).
- Drive CLK below 200 kHz (or stop it) to sleep; with SELECT high the DATA pin is then pulled to GND, with SELECT low it floats (ds p. 16).

## Layout notes

- Recommended land (Figure 14, ds p. 18), 1:1 with the package pads: four 0.522 × 0.725 mm signal pads, Ø1.625 mm outer / Ø1.025 mm inner GND ring. Signal-pad centres are 0.822 mm apart across the pair and 1.675 mm apart along the package; the ring centre is 1.252 mm from the nearer signal-pad column and 0.838 mm from the nearer signal-pad row (Figure 14, ds p. 18; Figure 16, ds p. 20).
- Stencil (Figure 15, ds p. 18): 0.422 × 0.625 mm signal apertures; ring Ø1.625 / Ø1.125 mm split by four 0.1 mm bridges. Keep paste off the sound hole (ds p. 18).
- PCB sound hole: not smaller than the 0.375 mm package port; 0.5–1 mm diameter recommended (ds p. 18).
- Library `FP_TDK_T3902`: 0.725 × 0.522 mm signal pads (the source pad turned 90° with the whole land), Ø1.625/Ø1.025 mm GND annulus, segmented-annulus paste Ø1.625/Ø1.125 mm with 0.1 mm gaps, 0.5 mm non-plated hole at the ring centre. Pad centres: (±0.8375, 1.364) and (±0.8375, 0.542) mm, ring at (0, −0.710) mm. See the check below.
- Geometry check (computed from Figure 16, ds p. 20): with the package centred at the origin, the signal-pad centres sit ±0.8375 mm from one centre line (half of 1.675 mm) and 0.954 ± 0.411 mm along the other (0.822 mm pair spacing), and the ring centre sits 0.708 mm from the centre on the opposite side. Turned 90° into the library's frame that is pads at x = ±0.8375 mm, y = +0.954 ± 0.411 mm (0.543 and 1.365 mm), ring at y = −0.708 mm. The library matches to within 0.002 mm.
- Do not pick the part up by its bottom (port) side, and do not pull or blow air through the port (ds p. 19). Do not wash with water reaching the port, blow-off or ultrasonic cleaning (ds p. 19).

## Pitfalls

- SELECT must be tied, not left open: the source defines only GND and VDD (Table 10, ds p. 12). The library declares `SELECT` optional.
- A clock outside the mode bands (for example 0.8–1.0 MHz or 3.3–4.1 MHz) is not specified (ds p. 6).
- Changing between low-power and standard or high-performance modes takes 10 ms (ds p. 6); discard data during the switch.
- In sleep with SELECT high the microphone pulls DATA low (ds p. 16), which corrupts a shared DATA line if the other microphone is still running.

## Not established by the sources

- **Supply minimum:** the library header says 1.62–3.63 V and the `VDD` comment says "1.62-3.63V". The source gives 1.65 V minimum (ds p. 4).
- **Courtyard:** the library courtyard is 3.15 × 4.0 mm centred at y = +0.327 mm, so it runs from y = −1.673 to +2.327 mm. The package (2.65 × 3.50 mm, ds p. 20) is centred on the origin in this frame (computed above) and runs from y = −1.75 to +1.75 mm, so the body overhangs the courtyard by about 0.08 mm on the sound-port side. This is computed from the drawing, not stated by the source.
- **Hole size:** the library's 0.5 mm non-plated hole is at the low end of the recommended 0.5–1 mm (ds p. 18); the source does not say whether the hole may be plated.
- **Mounting rules for the acoustic path** (gaskets, sealing, port tube) are in application notes AN-1003 and AN-1124 (ds p. 17), which are not in the repository.
- **ESD rating:** the source gives only an ESD caution, no HBM or CDM value (ds p. 8).
- **MSL:** not stated in the datasheet.
