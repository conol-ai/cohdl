## Summary

The L76K is a GNSS module that receives GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS and QZSS, with AGNSS, an internal LNA and SAW filter, a 26 MHz TCXO and a 32.768 kHz RTC crystal (ds p. 10, p. 11). It runs from 2.7–3.4 V and draws 29 mA when acquiring or tracking GPS + BeiDou (ds p. 10). It talks NMEA 0183 4.1 and Quectel's CASIC protocol over one UART at 9600 bps by default (ds p. 12, p. 17). It accepts a passive or an active antenna; VDD_RF supplies and detects an active antenna (ds p. 22). A backup supply on V_BCKP keeps the RTC and backup RAM alive for hot starts (ds p. 16, p. 19). The module is 10.1 × 9.7 × 2.0 mm with 18 LCC pads (ds p. 10).

## Typical application

- Power: an LDO of at least 100 mA output on VCC, with 10 µF, 100 nF and a TVS diode near the VCC pad (ds p. 16). The module has no over-voltage or reverse-polarity protection (ds p. 24).
- Backup: a coin cell (> 20 µA capability) or a rechargeable cell / super capacitor on V_BCKP with 4.7 µF and 100 nF near the pad (ds p. 16, p. 18, p. 19). Without V_BCKP the backup RAM is lost when VCC goes off and hot start is not possible (ds p. 16, p. 19).
- Reset: pull RESET_N low for at least 10 ms; Quectel recommends an NPN open-collector driver (4.7 kΩ base, 47 kΩ base-emitter) (Figure 7, ds p. 19).
- Standby: pull WAKE_UP low to enter, release to return to Full on (ds p. 18).
- Default configuration at power-on: GPS + BeiDou, 9600 bps, NMEA RMC/VTG/GGA/GSA/GSV/GLL/TXT/ZDA, 1 Hz, AGNSS on (ds p. 17). `$PCAS04` changes the constellations, not kept over power-off (ds p. 18).
- UART: TXD to MCU RX, RXD to MCU TX, common ground; 9600–115200 bps, no hardware flow control (Figure 9, ds p. 20).
- Passive antenna: 50 Ω line to RF_IN with a reserved pi match, C1 and C2 not fitted, R1 = 0 Ω (Figure 12, ds p. 23).
- Active antenna with detection: C1 100 pF DC block, pi match R1 0 Ω with C2/C3 not fitted, bias from VDD_RF through L1 47 nH with C4 100 nF; place L1 and C4 near the antenna connector, with L1's near pad on the RF trace (Figure 10, ds p. 22).
- Active antenna without detection: ANTON switches the antenna supply through an NPN/PMOS stage with R2 10 kΩ, R3 22 Ω, L1 47 nH (Figure 11, ds p. 23).
- Antenna: 1559–1609 MHz, VSWR < 2, RHCP; passive gain > 0 dBi; active antenna NF < 1.5 dB and total gain < 17 dB (Table 7, ds p. 21).

## Layout notes

- Recommended land (Figure 15, ds p. 28): 1.80 × 0.80 mm rounded lands (0.90 mm from each land centre to its ends), 1.1 mm pitch, 9.70 mm between row centres, 7.90 mm inner and 11.50 mm outer span, end lands 0.65 mm from the 10.10 mm body edge. `FP_Quectel_L76K_18LCC_101x97mm` puts 1.8 × 0.8 mm oval pads at x = ±4.85 mm and y = −4.4 … +4.4 mm in 1.1 mm steps, which gives the same spans. It matches.
- Module (Figures 13–14, ds pp. 26–27): 10.10 ± 0.15 × 9.70 ± 0.15 × 2.0 ± 0.20 mm; castellated pads 0.80 mm wide at 1.10 mm pitch, 0.87 mm deep, end pads 0.65 mm from the body edge.
- Keep at least 3 mm between the module and other components (Figure 15 note, ds p. 28).
- RF_IN: 50 Ω, as short as possible (ds p. 21, p. 23).
- MSL 3; floor life 168 h at 23 ± 5 °C, < 60 % RH; bake 8 h at 120 °C otherwise (ds p. 30).
- Stencil 0.15–0.18 mm; reflow peak 238–246 °C, never above 246 °C, one reflow only; place the module after the first side has been reflowed (ds p. 31, p. 32).

## Pitfalls

- The VCC range is 2.7–3.4 V (ds p. 25), narrower than a typical 3.3 V ± 10 % rail; 3.6 V is the absolute limit (ds p. 24).
- All I/O thresholds scale with VCC (ds pp. 14–15). There is no separate I/O supply; a 1.8 V host needs level shifting.
- VDD_RF outputs VCC itself (ds p. 22); check the active antenna's voltage range, and keep its current between 2.5 and 50 mA or the module reports open or short (ds p. 22).
- SET floating selects BeiDou + GPS, low selects GPS + GLONASS (ds p. 15).
- All RESERVED and unused pins must be left open (ds p. 15).
- Solder GND before RF_IN, and do not touch RF_IN with charged objects (ds p. 25).

## Not established by the sources

The library pad numbers, names and land geometry agree with the manual (Figure 2 and Table 4, ds pp. 13–15; Figure 15, ds p. 28). Gaps and disagreements:

- **Source URL:** the README gives only `https://www.quectel.com`, Quectel's home page; the manual gives no document URL.
- **Ordering code:** the manual names the product and its reel "L76K" (Table 11, ds p. 33) and gives no longer ordering code. The library MPN `L76K` matches that, but the source does not say whether distributors' suffixed parts are the same item.
- **VDD_RF vs VCC_RF:** the pin table calls pad 14 `VDD_RF` (ds p. 15); the block diagram and internal power figure call it `VCC_RF` (ds p. 11, p. 16).
- **Pin type of SET:** Table 4 gives SET no I/O type (ds p. 15). The library types it `input`. Its internal pull state is not stated, only that floating selects BeiDou + GPS.
- **No ESD rating** and no RF_IN DC limit are given (ds pp. 24–25).
- **RESERVED16/17 comments:** `l76k.cohdl` gives no page for RESERVED16 and RESERVED17; Table 4 lists pins 15, 16 and 17 as RESERVED (ds p. 15).
- **Courtyard:** the library courtyard is the body plus the 3 mm keep-out, which the manual states as a clearance (ds p. 28), not as a courtyard.
