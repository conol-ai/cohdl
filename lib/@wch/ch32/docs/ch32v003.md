# CH32V003F4P6

QingKe V2A RISC-V, up to 48 MHz, 16 KB flash and 2 KB SRAM, TSSOP20.
Use `wch_ch32::MCU_CH32V003F4P6`.
Primary evidence: WCH CH32V003DS0 V1.8, section 2.1, Table 2-1 and
section 4.1; see [sources](sources.md).

## Connections

- VDD is pin 9 and VSS is pin 7. Use local supply bypassing and the voltage,
  ramp and reset requirements in the datasheet. Do not connect a 5 V supply
  to an attached 3.3 V peripheral without checking both interfaces.
- PD1 pin 18 is the single-wire SWIO/SDI programming/debug terminal. Expose
  it with ground and a supply reference for a compatible WCH-Link setup.
  There is no separate SWCLK terminal or BOOT0 pin on this device.
- PD7 pin 4 multiplexes NRST; reset behavior depends on the option-byte
  configuration. This is not a separate dedicated input pin in the model.
- PA1/PA2 pins 5/6 multiplex OSCI/OSCO. Keep them available if the application
  requires an external oscillator; an internal oscillator is available.
- Default peripheral routes include USART TX/RX on PD5/PD6 (2/3), I2C
  SDA/SCL on PC1/PC2 (11/12), and SPI SCK/MOSI/MISO on PC5/PC6/PC7 (15/16/17).
  Remapped alternatives must be checked against Table 2-1 and firmware.
- There is no USB controller. A USB connector requires another interface
  device or a separately designed software implementation.

## Land pattern

`soic::KICAD_TSSOP_20_4_4X6_5MM_P0_65MM` from `soic 0.2.0` matches the
nominal 4.4 x 6.5 mm body, 6.4 mm lead span, 0.65 mm pitch and 20 pins in
section 4.1. Pin 1 is upper-left and pin 20 upper-right in component-side
view. This TSSOP has no exposed pad; the QFN variant's pad 0 is not present.

Copper is 1.475 x 0.4 mm with 0.1 mm corner radius at x = +/-2.8625 mm,
with 0.25 mm inter-pad gap and a 7.7 x 7.0 mm courtyard. Nominal lead tips
at +/-3.2 mm are within the copper's +/-2.125 to +/-3.6 mm extent.
This establishes nominal alignment only. The datasheet gives a broad
greater-of-0.2-mm-or-10-percent tolerance note rather than a PCB land pattern;
full worst-case lead fit and assembly yield have not been established.
See the package [README](README.md) and dependency license for the qualification
boundary and KiCad attribution.
