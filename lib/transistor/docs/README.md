# Official source documents

`jscj_s8050.evidence.md` records the authorized manufacturer document, exact
ordering identity, pinout, electrical limits, SOT-23 dimensions and suggested
land used by `transistor::BJT_JSCJ_S8050_J3Y`.

## LCSC binding (transistor 0.2.1)

`BJT_JSCJ_S8050_J3Y` binds its primary to LCSC C2146 with a zero map: `supplier lcsc "C2146"`. The part number is in `tools/sourcing_data/lcsc_map.json` since the B2 full run, and its frame row `c2b1-sot23_s8050-c2146` cites C2 frame board 1 (`tools/sourcing_data/jlc_previews/2026-10-30-c2-board-1/`). That preview read a quarter turn on the land as it was before 0.2.0, which `FP_JSCJ_SOT23` has since been moved onto. No land changed from 0.2.0, so a plain `build` writes the same BOM and CPL. The three `transistor` frame rows now name 0.2.1.

## SOT-23 parts (`transistor` 0.2.2)

`src/sot23.cohdl` holds 45 SOT-23 bipolar transistors (`NPN_SOT23`, `PNP_SOT23`), generated by `tools/gen_sot23.py` from the manufacturers' sheets (evidence in `docs/sot23.evidence.md`). Every part sits on `sot::SOT3P190X290X130N`; the device's pins follow the sheet's numbered SOT-23 diagram.

### LCSC bindings (45 of the 45)

A polarized part binds only with its own preview reading: the assembler's model, with the binding's mapping, must put its pin 1 mark at our pad 1 on four copies (top and bottom, 0 and 37 degrees). The readings are preview boards 15 and 18 (`tools/sourcing_data/jlc_previews/2026-11-23-board-*`); each binding's frame row (`tools/frame_boards/sot23_frames.py`) cites its record, and its part number is the map row of the 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24 backfills (`tools/sourcing_data/lcsc_backfill/2026-11-23-sot23/`, `2026-11-24-sot23-2/`). 45 parts are bound: 44 with no mapping, 1 with `rotate 90`. MMBTA56 (Zhuhai Hongjiacheng) is the one turned a quarter turn (board 15), read at pad 1 with `rotate 90` on board 18.
