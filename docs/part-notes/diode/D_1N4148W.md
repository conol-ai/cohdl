## Summary

The 1N4148W-7-F is a 100 V, 300 mA silicon switching diode in SOD123, sold on 3,000-piece tape and reel (ds p. 1). Choose it for fast signal switching, clamping and steering. Its reverse recovery is 4.0 ns max and its capacitance 2.0 pF max (ds p. 2). It is not a rectifier or power diode: the 400 mW dissipation rating assumes very large copper pads (ds p. 2).

## Typical application

- The source asks for no external parts and gives no application circuit (ds pp. 1-5).
- Signal switching, steering and clamping up to 100 V reverse (ds p. 2). Budget 0.715 V at 1 mA up to 1.25 V at 150 mA forward drop (ds p. 2).
- Use the typical forward curves (Figure 2) and reverse-leakage curves vs. temperature (Figure 3) for estimates. Do not design to them: they are typical only (ds p. 3).

## Layout notes

- Suggested SOD123 lands: X = 0.900 mm, Y = 0.950 mm, X1 = 4.050 mm (ds p. 4). `FP_D_SOD_123` matches these values (pads 0.9 x 0.95 mm at ±1.575 mm).
- Package body: E = 2.55-2.85 mm, He = 3.55-3.85 mm, D = 1.40-1.70 mm, A = 1.00-1.35 mm (ds p. 4).
- The 400 mW / 315 °C/W figures assume 2" x 2" pads with 3 oz copper (Note 5, ds p. 2). The source gives no rating for the suggested small lands.

## Pitfalls

- Polarity: the cathode is the band end (ds p. 1). Library pad 1 is Cathode. The datasheet does not number the pads, so check orientation against the band, not against pin numbers from another SOD-123 vendor.
- Derating: Figure 1 holds PD = 400 mW up to TA = 25 °C, then falls linearly to 0 at 150 °C (ds p. 3). 400 mW is not available at elevated ambient.
- IFM 300 mA is a continuous rating; the 2.0 A surge applies only to a non-repetitive 1.0 µs pulse (ds p. 2).
- tRR = 4.0 ns max holds only for the stated test (10 mA forward/reverse, 100 Ω, ds p. 2). Other drive conditions are not characterised.
- Leakage rises from 1.0 µA at 75 V/25 °C to 50 µA at 75 V/150 °C (ds p. 2). Account for this in high-impedance or sample-and-hold nodes.
- This datasheet also covers BAV16W, and the 1N4148WQ is the AEC-Q101 automotive grade. The 1N4148W-7-F is not the Q part (ds p. 1).

## Not established by the sources

- No recommended operating conditions table exists. The only temperature row is the combined operating and storage range from Thermal Characteristics (ds p. 2).
- The ratings and characteristics tables are shared by BAV16W and 1N4148W, with no per-part columns (ds p. 2). The values above apply to 1N4148W only to the extent the datasheet does not distinguish them.
- No typical values are given for VF, IR, CT or tRR. Only maxima are given, plus typical curves (ds pp. 2-3).
- Marking code "T4" is listed without saying which of the two part numbers carries it (ds p. 1).
- Not given: RθJA or PD for the suggested minimum-pad layout, junction-to-case/lead resistance, ESD rating, forward recovery time.
