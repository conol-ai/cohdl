## Summary

The ST25R3916 is an NFC front end for reader/writer, card emulation and peer-to-peer, EMVCo 3.0 compliant, supporting NFC-A/B up to 848 kbit/s, FeliCa up to 424 kbit/s and NFC-V up to 53 kb/s (ds p. 1). It drives the antenna directly from two RFO outputs, single-ended or differential, and has low-power capacitive and inductive card detection (ds p. 1, pp. 13–14). It runs from 2.4–5.5 V (2.6 V minimum below −20 °C) with a separate 1.65–5.5 V VDD_IO for the host interface (ds p. 137). The host interface is SPI (up to 10 Mbit/s) or I²C (up to 3.4 MHz, address 50h), selected by the I2C_EN pin (ds p. 1, p. 48, p. 54). It needs a 27.12 MHz crystal (ds p. 34). The -AQET suffix is the −40 to 105 °C grade in the UFQFPN32 package with non-wettable flanks, on tape and reel (ds p. 156).

## Typical application

- Minimum systems for single-ended and differential antenna driving, both with EMC filter, are Figures 1 and 2 (ds pp. 13–14). Component values for the EMC filter and matching network are not in the data sheet.
- Supplies: VDD and VDD_TX must come from the same supply (ds p. 39). VDD_IO sets the host-pin level, 1.65–5.5 V, and may be above or below VDD (ds p. 39).
- Supply mode: the default is 5 V mode; set bit sup3V after power-up when VDD is 3.3 V (max 3.6 V) (ds p. 40).
- Regulator decoupling: 2.2 µF ∥ 10 nF on each regulator pin, 1 µF ∥ 10 nF on AGDC (ds p. 40); VDD_AM needs 2.2 µF + 1 nF (ds p. 41).
- VDD_DR is the driver supply input; in normal use the driver runs from VDD_RF (Figures 1–2, ds pp. 13–14). Above 350 mArms of driver current the VDD_RF regulator cannot be used and VDD_RF and VDD_DR must be tied to VDD_TX (ds p. 41).
- VDD_A and VDD_D regulators are mandatory in 5 V mode; in 3.3 V mode VDD_D may be disabled and fed by shorting VDD_A to VDD_D (bit vspd_off) (ds p. 41).
- Interface: I2C_EN to GND for SPI, to VDD_D for I²C (ds p. 13, p. 48). SPI is CPOL = 0, CPHA = 1 with active-low BSS (ds p. 48). MOSI and MISO may be shorted for 3-wire SPI (ds p. 49).
- Crystal: 27.12 MHz between XTI and XTO; the oscillator regulates the XTI amplitude to 1 VPP (ds p. 34).
- MCU_CLK supplies 3.39 MHz by default (13.56 / 6.78 / 3.39 MHz selectable) and a 32 kHz clock when the crystal is off (ds p. 48).

## Layout notes

- ST's UFQFPN32 footprint example (Figure 39, ds p. 153), measured on the rendered figure: lands 0.60 × 0.30 mm at 0.50 mm pitch; 5.30 mm across the outer land edges; 3.80 mm is the span of each 8-land row (7 × 0.50 + 0.30 mm), and 0.75 mm is the distance from the end of a side row to the outer edge of the adjacent row; exposed land 3.45 × 3.45 mm. Land centres are therefore at ±2.35 mm, inner land edges at ±2.05 mm.
- Package (Table 135, ds p. 153): D = E = 4.90–5.10 mm, exposed pad D2 = E2 3.40–3.60 mm, e = 0.50 mm, L = 0.30–0.50 mm, b = 0.18–0.30 mm, A = 0.50–0.60 mm.
- Solder the exposed pad to PCB ground, and solder all leads (Figure 38 notes, ds p. 152).
- Operation at temperature requires a PCB with sufficient heat dissipation (ds p. 137).

## Pitfalls

- I2C_EN is a 3 V-domain pin (max 3.6 V operating) and must be pulled to VDD_D for I²C, not to a 5 V VDD (ds p. 21, p. 55, p. 137).
- Pins 2–5, 7, 18–20 and 22–25 are 3 V-domain pins: 3.6 V operating maximum even when VDD is 5 V (ds p. 137).
- The part powers up in 5 V supply mode; on 3.3 V set sup3V (ds p. 40).
- VDD_RF, VDD_AM, VDD_A, VDD_D and AGDC are regulator outputs that need their capacitors even though the library marks them optional (ds p. 40, p. 41).
- IRQ is active high (ds p. 49, p. 55).
- The I²C address 50h is fixed (ds p. 54).

## Not established by the sources

Pad numbers, names and pin-1 location agree with the data sheet (Figure 4 and Table 2, ds pp. 19–21). Disagreements and gaps, most serious first:

- **Land length and position (`QFN32N50P500X500_1EP345X345`):** the library comment reads Figure 39 as "5.30 mm outside span, 3.80 mm inside span … 0.75 x 0.30 mm lands", and places 0.75 × 0.30 mm lands centred at ±2.275 mm (inner edges ±1.90 mm). On the rendered Figure 39 (ds p. 153), 3.80 mm is dimensioned from the outer edge of pad 32 to the outer edge of pad 25 (the length of one 8-land row), and the land is dimensioned 0.60 mm long next to pad 25; 0.75 mm runs from the end of the pad 17–24 row to the outer edge of pad 16. ST's lands are therefore 0.60 × 0.30 mm centred at ±2.35 mm (inner edges ±2.05 mm). The library's lands reach 0.15 mm further toward the centre, leaving 0.175 mm to the 3.45 mm exposed land instead of 0.325 mm. The outer edge (±2.65 mm), pitch, land width and exposed land match.
- **Operating temperature for UFQFPN32:** Table 119 gives an ambient range only for VFQFPN32 (−40 to 105 °C) and WLCSP36 (−40 to 85 °C) (ds p. 137). The ordering scheme makes "A" −40 to 105 °C and "QE" the UFQFPN32 (ds p. 156), but the operating table does not name UFQFPN32.
- **Source URL:** the README gives only `https://www.st.com`; neither it nor the PDF gives a document URL.
- **Optional supply pins:** the library marks VDD_TX and VDD_DR `optional`. VDD_TX must be connected to the same supply as VDD (ds p. 39), and VDD_DR is the antenna driver supply input (ds p. 21). The regulator outputs VDD_D, VDD_A, VDD_RF and VDD_AM are also `optional` though each needs decoupling (ds pp. 40–41).
- **Thermal pad:** Table 2 lists pin 33 only as "Thermal pad", type P (ds p. 21); the ground connection comes from the package note (ds p. 152). The library names it `EP`, `required power_in`, which agrees with that note.
- **MISO:** typed `bidirectional` in the library; it is a tri-state output in SPI and the I²C data line in I²C mode (ds p. 21). IRQ is typed `output`; its drive type is not stated beyond "Digital output" (ds p. 49).
- **Not given:** crystal ESR / load-capacitance limits, EMC filter and matching values, thermal resistance, and a CDM ESD rating.
- **Another footprint in the repository:** `lib/qfn/src/footprints.cohdl` also has an "ST25R3916 … VFQFPN32" footprint (`QFN32N50P500X500_1EP34X34`, 0.85 × 0.30 mm lands). The NFC part does not use it and it was not checked here.
