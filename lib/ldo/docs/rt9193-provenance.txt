RT9193 — Richtek 300mA LDO, SOT-23-5
========================================

Pinout (SOT-23-5): 1=IN, 2=GND, 3=EN, 4=BP, 5=OUT.

Output voltage variants: RT9193-15 (1.5V), RT9193-28 (2.8V).

Datasheet: DS9193-18 (June 2022), committed locally as rt9193.pdf
(SHA-256 bbab3a6dcb12085e8b2988d10d25f34a9fad4b3a5e1cdbcdb7b4314efc55f43f).
Official source:
https://www.richtek.com/assets/product_file/RT9193/DS9193-18.pdf

Used in the three-controller watch design to supply OV5640 camera rails:
- RT9193-15 -> DVDD 1.5V
- RT9193-28 -> AVDD 2.8V
