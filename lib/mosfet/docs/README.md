## Coverage and geometry

- Page 1 supplies the top-view terminal map and the two exact tape-and-reel
  ordering codes: pin 1 gate, pin 2 source, and pin 3 drain.
- Page 6 supplies the SOT523 outline and suggested pad layout. The footprint
  is a 90-degree rotation of that drawing and preserves the official
  0.40 x 0.51 mm lands, 1.29 mm row separation, and 1.00 mm lead pitch.
- The courtyard covers both the maximum package outline and every copper land;
  the footprint includes an explicit pin-1 mark.
- No separate manufacturer CAD file was used; the footprint is derived from
  the dimensioned manufacturer land-pattern drawing.

The PDF passed `pdfinfo`, Poppler text extraction, and full-page rendering.
Pages 1 and 6 were visually inspected for identity, exact ordering codes,
terminal map, package orientation, and land dimensions.

## SOT-23 parts (`mosfet` 0.1.1)

`src/sot23.cohdl` holds 29 SOT-23 MOSFETs (`NMOS_SOT23`, `PMOS_SOT23`), generated by `tools/gen_sot23.py` from the manufacturers' sheets (evidence in `docs/sot23.evidence.md`). Every part sits on `sot::SOT3P190X290X130N`; the device's pins follow the sheet's numbered SOT-23 diagram.

### LCSC bindings (29 of the 29)

A polarized part binds only with its own preview reading: the assembler's model, with the binding's mapping, must put its pin 1 mark at our pad 1 on four copies (top and bottom, 0 and 37 degrees). The readings are preview boards 16 and 18 (`tools/sourcing_data/jlc_previews/2026-11-23-board-*`); each binding's frame row (`tools/frame_boards/sot23_frames.py`) cites its record, and its part number is the map row of the 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24 backfills (`tools/sourcing_data/lcsc_backfill/2026-11-23-sot23/`, `2026-11-24-sot23-2/`). 29 parts are bound: 27 with no mapping, 2 with `rotate 90`. The two turned ones are Zhuhai Hongjiacheng HL2302 and HL3407A, drawn a quarter turn against the land (board 16) and read at pad 1 with `rotate 90` on board 18.
