## Summary

The RT9080 is a PMOS LDO with an active-high enable, 1.2–5.5 V input, 600 mA output and 2 µA typical quiescent current (ds p. 1, p. 6, p. 12). Both library parts are the fixed 3.3 V option without the SNS pin ("RT9080", not "RT9080N"), Richtek Green: -33GJ5 is TSOT-23-5 and -33GQZ is ZQFN-4L 1x1 (ds p. 1). Choose it for an always-on or switched 3.3 V rail from a 5 V or single-cell source where standby current matters: 0.5 µA max in shutdown (ds p. 6). It needs ≥ 1 µF output capacitance (ds p. 4, p. 12). Do not use it above 5.5 V input (6.5 V absolute, ds p. 5). At 3.3 V output, 600 mA needs up to 0.53 V of headroom (ds p. 6). The 1x1 package can dissipate only about 0.42 W at 25 °C (ds p. 14).

## Typical application

- CIN = 1 µF from VIN to GND, close to VIN and GND (ds p. 4, p. 7, p. 13).
- COUT ≥ 1 µF ceramic at VOUT. The minimum effective capacitance is quoted as 0.7 µF (ds p. 4) and as ≥ 1 µF (ds p. 7, p. 12). Size for ≥ 1 µF after DC bias and temperature. ESR must be ≥ 1 mΩ (ds p. 13). Tantalum is also stable (ds p. 1).
- EN: tie to VIN for always-on (ds p. 13), or drive it from logic: off below 0.4 V (ds p. 6). Never leave it floating, because the input is CMOS (ds p. 13).
- TSOT pad 4 (NC): leave open or connect to GND to extend copper (ds p. 4).
- Headroom at 3.3 V: VIN ≥ 3.3 V + 0.53 V ≈ 3.83 V for 600 mA worst case (ds p. 6). Electrical limits are characterised for VIN > VOUT + 1 V = 4.3 V (ds p. 6).
- Dissipation: PD(MAX) = (125 °C − TA) / θJA(EVB) (ds p. 14). That gives 0.99 W for TSOT-23-5 and 0.42 W for ZQFN-4L at TA = 25 °C (ds p. 14). For example, 5 V to 3.3 V at 600 mA is about 1.0 W, which is over the ZQFN limit and at the TSOT limit even at 25 °C.

## Layout notes

- TSOT-23-5 land (section 20.1): P1 = 0.95 mm, P2 = 1.90 mm, A = 3.60 mm, B = 1.60 mm, C = 1.00 mm, D = 0.70 mm, M = 2.60 mm, ±0.10 mm (ds p. 19). Library `SOT5P95X290X160N` uses 1.0 x 0.7 mm pads at x = ±1.3 mm (3.6 mm span, 1.6 mm gap) and y = 0, ±0.95 mm. That is section 20.1 rotated 90°, so it matches.
- ZQFN-4L land (section 20.2): P = 0.625, Ax = 1.800, Ay = 0.900, Bx = 0.450, By = 0.350, C = 0.675, C1 = 0.474, D = 0.275, D1 = 0.074, Sx = Sy = 0.400 mm, ±0.050 mm (ds p. 20). Library `FP_Richtek_ZQFN4L_1X1` reproduces these values, including the 45° inner-corner cuts and the diamond SGND land.
- Put the input capacitor as close as possible to minimise the power loop, keep power traces short, and avoid vias in the CIN/COUT connections (ds p. 15). Use thermal vias to a ground plane for the TSOT (Figure 7, ds p. 16). Route EN to its source by via on the ZQFN (Figure 6, ds p. 16).
- Solder the exposed pad to a large GND copper area (ds p. 4).

## Pitfalls

- The two packages have different pad maps (pad 1 is VIN on TSOT-23-5 and VOUT on ZQFN-4L, ds p. 4). Use the footprint bound to each part.
- VOUT must not exceed VIN by more than 0.3 V (absolute maximum, ds p. 5). Back-feeding the output, e.g. from another supply or a large COUT after VIN collapses, is outside the ratings.
- Over-temperature protection trips only when the junction exceeds 150 °C and the output current exceeds 30 mA (ds p. 12). It is not a guard at light load.
- The EN on-threshold is given only as "0.9 V min" (ds p. 6), with no maximum. Drive EN from a rail-level logic high or tie it to VIN (ds p. 13). Do not rely on a 1.0 V signal.
- The shutdown output discharge (about 80 Ω, ds p. 6, p. 12) pulls VOUT down when EN is low. Do not use it as a power-path element shared with another source.
- The 2 µA IQ is at no load (ds p. 6). These fixed parts need no feedback divider. Do not copy the RT9080N adjustable circuit (Figure 2, ds p. 7), whose divider should carry more than 50 µA (ds p. 13). These parts have no SNS pin (ds p. 1).

## Not established by the sources

- **Checker coverage:** the ZQFN-4L pin map is not machine-checked (see note at top). It was checked by hand against ds p. 4 and `rt9080.cohdl` `pins[ZQFN4L]`, and they agree.
- **"Exposed pad" on TSOT-23-5:** the shared GND row says "The exposed pad must be soldered to a large PCB and connected to GND" for both packages (ds p. 4). TSOT-23-5 has no exposed pad in its outline (ds p. 17). The library comment on the TSOT `GND` pin repeats "EP must solder to PCB (p4)", which does not apply to that package.
- **COUT requirement conflict:** "0.7µF or greater effective capacitance" (ds p. 4), "Effective Capacitance ≥ 1µF" (ds p. 7), and "minimum output capacitance … 1µF, considering the temperature and voltage coefficient" (ds p. 12). The source does not say which governs.
- **EN thresholds:** VEN_R has only a 0.9 V minimum (ds p. 6), while the text says "a VEN above 0.9V turns the regulator on" (ds p. 12). No guaranteed logic-high level is given, and no thresholds over temperature. The source states no internal EN pull-up or pull-down, only that EN must not float (ds p. 13).
- **ZQFN pin-1 tape orientation:** with no suffix (as in RT9080-33GQZ) the order code means "Quadrant 1 or Quadrant 3" (ds p. 1). The packing pages show only Quadrant 1 and Quadrant 2 (ds pp. 22–23). The pick-and-place rotation for the bound MPN is not determined by the source.
- **SGND connection:** the recommended land isolates the SGND diamond from all four leads (ds p. 20), and SGND must connect to the GND plane (ds p. 4). Neither the land pattern nor Figure 6 (ds p. 16) shows how, e.g. via-in-pad or trace. The land diamond (0.400 mm diagonals) is smaller than the package's exposed pad (D2/E2 0.45–0.55 mm, ds p. 18).
- **θJA(EVB) board:** Note 6 says θJA(EVB) is measured on a four-layer 70 x 50 mm board (ds p. 5). Section 18.4 says the ZQFN θJA(EVB) = 236 °C/W is on a two-layer Richtek EVB, and calls the TSOT board "standard JEDEC 51-7" (ds p. 14).
- **Datasheet errata:** the Figure 1 caption reads "with Sense Function (RT9080) / without Sense Function (RT9080N)" (ds p. 7), which is the reverse of the ordering information (ds p. 1). The pin table prints EN as "1EN" (ds p. 4). Line regulation is given in "%" without a per-volt basis (ds p. 6).
- **Footprint name:** `SOT5P95X290X160N` encodes 2.90 / 1.60 mm, which are body length and width (D 2.692–3.099, B 1.397–1.803 mm). The lead span is C 2.591–3.000 mm and the height is A 0.700–1.000 mm (ds p. 17). The pads themselves follow ds p. 19.
- **Not given:** marking codes ("contact our sales representative", ds p. 1), electrical limits over temperature (25 °C only, ds p. 6), start-up time and inrush, PSRR at VOUT = 3.3 V, maximum COUT, CDM ESD rating, and any reverse-current blocking.
