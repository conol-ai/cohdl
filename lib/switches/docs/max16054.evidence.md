# Analog Devices/Maxim MAX16054AZT+T evidence

- Manufacturer: Maxim Integrated Products (now Analog Devices).
- Exact order code: `MAX16054AZT+T`.
- Manufacturer datasheet:
  <https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/MAX16054.pdf>.
- Retrieved document sha256:
  `7dfb9962d107623f1d618a3e66a93484f12c724e3b5160cfa85c5d5297f248b0`.
- Pin configuration, page 1: 1 IN, 2 GND, 3 CLEAR, 4 complementary active-low
  output, 5 OUT, 6 VCC.
- Function: momentary normally-open switch input, 50 ms typical internal
  debounce and latched push-on/push-off output. VCC range is 2.7 to 5.5 V;
  undervoltage lockout guarantees OUT low at power-up.
- Package: six-pin Thin SOT23, package code Z6-1. The footprint uses KiCad's
  audited nominal `SOT-23-6.kicad_mod` geometry, sha256
  `84743605d3330f6c4825d699837b02d3db0b862006bc4e2d57ee55f55eec54a0`:
  1.325 x 0.60 mm lands, 0.95 mm lead pitch and 2.275 mm opposing row centres.
- Qualification boundary: `CLEAR` must not float, and the complementary output
  is not interchangeable with `OUT`. The consuming design must verify the
  load-switch enable polarity and supply rail remains within 2.7 to 5.5 V.
