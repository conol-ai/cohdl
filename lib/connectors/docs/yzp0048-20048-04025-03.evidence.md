## Recommended lands and implementation

All dimensions and coordinates are in millimetres. The layout gives
4 x diameter 2.20 lands with 4 x diameter 0.80 holes at 2.50 pitch. The
holes take the diameter 0.60, 0.70 long tails on the mounting face (side
view), which are the electrical terminals, so they are plated. Contact x
follows `2.5 * (n - 2.5)` for contacts 1 through 4.

| Pad(s) | Recommended PCB layout | Implementation | Maximum deviation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-4 | diameter 2.20 land, diameter 0.80 hole, 2.50 pitch | plated through-hole, 2.20 circle, 0.80 drill, x = -3.75, -1.25, 1.25, 3.75 | 0.00 |

The drawing does not number the contacts; the library keeps P1 to P4 from
left to right in this view (P1/P2 power, P3 data/ID, P4 not connected).

The housing is 21.20 wide and 9.17 deep: a 6.86 high oval (R3.43) centred
on the contact row and a 5.03 wide locating tab on one side, so the body
spans y -3.43 to +5.74. The courtyard is that body plus 0.25 per side:
21.70 x 9.67 centred at (0, 1.155). The magnets at both ends have no PCB
features.
