## Summary

The RS2257 is a single-pole double-throw analog switch: COM connects to NC when IN is low and to NO when IN is high, with break-before-make switching (ds p. 1). It runs from 1.8 V to 5.5 V (ds p. 1, p. 4). It has 0.6 Ω typical, 1.2 Ω maximum on-resistance at 5 V and a 30 MHz typical −3 dB bandwidth (ds p. 1, p. 3, p. 4). The analog signal must stay between GND and V+ (ds p. 3). The terminals are diode-clamped to the supply rails (ds p. 2, note 2), so the part does not isolate a signal that is higher than its own supply. It is not suited to high-frequency or low-capacitance signal paths: the on-state capacitance is 350 pF typical (ds p. 4).

## Typical application

- Function: IN = 0 connects COM to NC; IN = 1 connects COM to NO (ds p. 1).
- Hold IN at a defined level; the source says unused digital inputs must be held "at VIO or GND" (ds p. 3, note 1). The part has no VIO pin; V+ is the supply (ds p. 1).
- Keep the signal on NO, NC and COM between 0 V and V+ (ds p. 3). A signal that can swing more than 0.3 V beyond the rails must be limited to 10 mA or less (ds p. 2, note 2).
- With a 1.8 V supply the source gives no IN thresholds. VINH and VINL are specified only at 5 V and 3.3 V (ds p. 3).
- The source shows no application circuit and no supply bypass capacitor.

## Layout notes

- SC70-6 recommended land (ds p. 10): 0.40 mm × 0.75 mm pads, 0.65 mm pitch, 1.9 mm between row centres, 1.30 mm across the outer pads of a row. `SOT6P65X210X125N` uses 0.75 × 0.40 mm pads at x = ±0.95 mm and 0.65 mm pitch, which is the same land turned 90°.
- SOT23-6 recommended land (ds p. 11): 0.69 mm × 0.99 mm pads, 0.95 mm pitch, 2.59 mm between row centres, 1.90 mm across the outer pads of a row. `SOT6P95X290X160N` uses 0.99 × 0.69 mm pads at x = ±1.295 mm and 0.95 mm pitch, which is the same land turned 90°.
- Body sizes: SC70-6 D 2.0–2.2 mm, E 1.15–1.35 mm, A 0.9–1.1 mm (ds p. 10); SOT23-6 D 2.82–3.02 mm, E 1.5–1.7 mm, A 1.05–1.25 mm (ds p. 11).
- The source gives no further layout guidance.

## Pitfalls

- Selecting the other throw does not isolate the switch from a signal above V+, or from any live signal when V+ is absent. The terminals are clamped to the rails (ds p. 2, note 2), and leakage is specified only with V+ = 1.8–5.5 V (ds p. 3).
- A 5 V signal through the switch on a 3.3 V supply is outside the analog signal range (0 to V+, ds p. 3).
- The on and off capacitances (350 pF and 80 pF typical, ds p. 4) load the signal heavily. They are typical values only.
- Timing, bandwidth, isolation and capacitance are typical values at 25 °C only. The source gives no maximums for them (ds p. 4).
- SC70-6 and SOT23-6 lands differ (0.65 mm vs 0.95 mm pitch, ds p. 10, p. 11). Use the footprint bound to each part.

## Not established by the sources

The library's pin numbers and names, pin-1 location and both lands agree with the source. Points to note:

- **Pin name:** the library calls pad 5 `VPLUS`; the datasheet calls it V+ (ds p. 1). Only the spelling differs.
- **Source URL:** the only official location is `https://www.run-ic.com` (package README, PDF footer). No document URL or publication date is printed.
- **SC70-6 thermal resistance:** the package-thermal-resistance list covers SOT23-5, SOT23-6, MSOP-10, SOIC-8, TSSOP-8, SOIC-14 and TSSOP-14, but not SOT363/SC70-6 (ds p. 2).
- **Operating temperature:** −40 °C to 125 °C is printed inside the absolute-maximum-ratings block, not in an operating-conditions table (ds p. 2). Features and the ordering table give the same range (ds p. 1, p. 2).
- **Datasheet text copied from other parts:** the tBBM test condition names VNO1, VNC1, VNO2 and VNC2, which this single switch does not have (ds p. 4). Note 1 refers to a VIO supply that the part does not have (ds p. 3). The input-leakage condition "VIN = VIO or 0" has the same problem (ds p. 3).
- **Measurement figures without data:** crosstalk, charge injection and total harmonic distortion have test circuits (ds pp. 8–9) but no values anywhere in the tables.
- **Not given:** maximum switching times, IN thresholds at 1.8 V, any internal pull on IN, behaviour with V+ = 0 V, power-up sequencing, operation with signals above V+, and a recommended bypass capacitor.
- **Marking:** RS2257XC6 is marked "2257X" and RS2257XH "2257", where X is a date code (ds p. 2).
