# GD32E230F8P6TR

64 KB flash, 8 KB SRAM, Cortex-M23 up to 72 MHz, TSSOP20.
Use `gigadevice_gd32::MCU_GD32E230F8P6TR`.
Primary evidence: GD32E230xx Rev 3.1, Figure 2-8, Table 2-11,
sections 3.4/4.2/5.7 and Table 6-1; see [sources](sources.md).

## Connections

- Connect VDD pin 16 and VDDA pin 5; VSS is pin 15. This package has no
  separate VSSA, VBAT, exposed pad, or USB pins.
- VDD operates at 1.8-3.6 V; VDDA needs at least 2.4 V when using the ADC.
  Figure 4-1 specifies 4.7 uF plus 100 nF per VDD, and 1 uF plus 10 nF on
  VDDA. Place these at the corresponding pins; the TSSOP20 returns to VSS.
- Establish BOOT0 pin 1 deliberately for the intended boot configuration.
  Boot selection also depends on option-byte configuration; do not assume a
  bootloader or recovery interface solely from the part name.
- PA13 pin 19 and PA14 pin 20 provide SWDIO and SWCLK. Expose NRST pin 4 and
  a ground/reference connection for programming and recovery.
- PF0/PF1 pins 2/3 can be oscillator pins or GPIO. Use one physical function
  per pin. The internal oscillator permits applications without a crystal.
- PA9 pin 17 and PA10 pin 18 can be remapped to PA11/PA12. They are not
  additional terminals: connect through PA9/PA10 in this model and configure
  SYSCFG_CFG0 in firmware. This remap does not add USB support.

## Land pattern

The binding uses `soic::KICAD_TSSOP_20_4_4X6_5MM_P0_65MM` from `soic 0.3.0`.
Table 5-7 specifies a 4.4 x 6.5 mm nominal body, 6.4 mm nominal lead span,
0.65 mm pitch and 20 terminals; maximum body is 4.5 x 6.6 mm and maximum
lead span is 6.6 mm. Pin 1 is upper-left, counting down the left side. Since soic 0.3.0 the land is
turned onto the assembler's frame (library plan W3), so on the board pin 1
sits at the left end of the lower row.

The generic land has 1.475 x 0.4 mm rounded rectangular copper at
y = +/-2.8625 mm, 0.65 mm pitch, a 7.2 mm copper outside span and
7.0 x 7.7 mm courtyard. Nominal lead tips at +/-3.2 mm lie within the
copper extent +/-2.125 to +/-3.6 mm. The copper leaves 0.25 mm between
neighbors. This is a nominal dimensional fit check, not a process simulation.

GigaDevice Figure 5-14 instead recommends 1.20 x 0.33 mm rectangular pads
with 5.90 mm row-center spacing. The selected generic land is **not that
recommendation**. Qualify the larger copper/paste with the assembler.
KiCad provenance and license remain in the dependency's `LICENSE.kicad.md`
and `docs/README.md`; geometry is not duplicated here.
