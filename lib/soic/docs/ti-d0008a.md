# TI D0008A SOIC-8 land

Source: [TI TLV90x2 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv9022.pdf),
SNOSDA3H, November 2025, PDF pages 68-69; drawing 4214825/C, February 2019.
Captured PDF SHA-256:
`874c1d4f5ec1c1b4bf6c714d3fe8623d57ad6bff18b83930e3894061a0701c8a`.

The drawing identifies inches as controlling. This implementation converts
its example land dimensions exactly at 25.4 mm/in, rather than using rounded
bracketed metric labels: 0.061 x 0.024 in lands become 1.5494 x 0.6096 mm,
0.213 in row spacing becomes 5.4102 mm, 0.050 in pitch becomes 1.27 mm,
and R0.002 in corners become R0.0508 mm. Pin 1 is upper left in top view;
numbering runs down the left row and up the right row. The footprint turns
that view 90 degrees counter-clockwise onto the assembler's frame (library
plan W3), so pin 1 lands at the left end of the lower row. There is no exposed pad.

The 7.4596 x 6 mm courtyard and pin-1 dot are authored additions. The courtyard
clears copper by at least 0.25 mm, the marker by 0.275 mm, and the maximum
body including 0.006 in mold flash per end by more than 0.25 mm.
Silk-to-copper clearance is 0.2652 mm. Mask/paste process qualification remains
with PCB fabrication and assembly; this land does not qualify a circuit.
