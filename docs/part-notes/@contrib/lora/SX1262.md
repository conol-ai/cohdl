## Summary

The SX1262 is a half-duplex sub-GHz transceiver for LoRa and (G)FSK, tuning continuously from 150 MHz to 960 MHz (ds p. 1, p. 18). Its high-power PA delivers up to +22 dBm (ds p. 21) and is supplied straight from the battery through VDD_IN, so output power falls with VBAT: +22 dBm needs VBAT ≥ 3.3 V and the power plateaus at about +17 dBm at 1.8 V (ds p. 30, p. 34). Receive current is 4.6 mA (LoRa 125 kHz, DC-DC) and sleep current 160–600 nA (ds p. 16). It is controlled over SPI (up to 16 MHz) with a mandatory BUSY line and three DIOs; DIO2 can drive an RF switch and DIO3 can supply a TCXO (ds p. 50, p. 54). The reference clock is a 32 MHz crystal or TCXO (ds p. 15, p. 24). The data sheet gives no RF matching network: the board designer must supply one.

## Typical application

- Supply option C (DC-DC for the core, PA from VBAT): VDD_IN to VBAT, 15 µH inductor between VREG (pin 7) and DCC_SW (pin 9); VBAT 1.8 to 3.7 V, 3.3 V minimum for +22 dBm (Figure 5-3, ds p. 34). LDO-only operation (option D) drops the 15 µH inductor and the 47 nH inductor "before pin 1" at slightly higher core current (ds p. 32, p. 35).
- DC-DC inductor: shielded, DCR ≤ 2 Ω, Idc ≥ 100 mA, self-resonance ≥ 20 MHz; Semtech lists Coilcraft LPS3010-153 and TDK MLZ2012N150L, MLZ2012M150W, VLS2010ET-150M, VLS2012ET-150M (Table 5-3, ds p. 35).
- Select DC-DC with SetRegulatorMode in STDBY_RC only (ds p. 32).
- Crystal: 32 MHz, no external load capacitors; the XTA/XTB trimming capacitors are internal, 11.3 pF to 33.4 pF in 0.47 pF steps (ds p. 23).
- TCXO (optional): clipped-sine output ≤ 1.2 Vpp into XTA through 220 Ω and 10 pF, XTB open, powered from DIO3 (1.6–3.3 V programmable, VBAT ≥ VTCXO + 200 mV), enabled with SetDIO3AsTCXOCtrl (Figure 4-2, ds p. 24).
- RF switch: DIO2 can drive it with SetDio2AsRfSwitchCtrl; DIO2 is high during TX and low otherwise (ds p. 54).
- Host interface: SPI mode 0 (CPOL = 0, CPHA = 0), BUSY is mandatory, and at least one DIO must be used for IRQ (ds p. 50). Wait for BUSY low before each command (ds p. 50, p. 52).
- Pull states: BUSY has a 20 kΩ pull-up in sleep; NRESET has a 50 kΩ pull-up from start-up; DIO1–3 have 50 kΩ pull-downs in reset and sleep (ds p. 52, p. 54).
- PA path: VR_PA is connected to RFO externally, through the board's matching network (ds p. 26). The data sheet gives no component values for it.
- Split supplies: VBAT may be 3.3 V for RF performance while VBAT_IO runs from a 1.8 V MCU supply, as long as VBAT_IO ≤ VBAT (Figure 5-5, ds p. 36).

## Layout notes

- Land pattern (Figure 16-3, ds p. 106): C = 4.00 mm centre-to-centre, G = 3.20 mm inside span, Z = 4.80 mm outside span, Y = 0.80 mm land length, X = 0.30 mm land width, P = 0.50 mm pitch, H = K = 2.70 mm exposed land. `QFN24N50P400X400_1EP270X270` puts 0.8 × 0.3 mm lands on ±2.0 mm centres at 0.5 mm pitch with a 2.7 mm square centre land, which gives the same 3.20 mm and 4.80 mm spans. It matches.
- Package (Figure 16-1, ds p. 105): 4.00 mm body, 0.50 mm pitch, terminal L 0.35–0.45 mm, b 0.18–0.30 mm, exposed pad D1 = E1 2.55–2.65 mm; the EP has a chamfer at the pin-1 corner.
- Thermal vias in the exposed land must go to a system ground plane (Figure 16-3 note 3, ds p. 106). The library footprint has none; its comment leaves them to the board layout.
- Keep the crystal thermally isolated from the transceiver, which heats up when transmitting (ds p. 22); Semtech's AN1200.37 is cited for heat-dissipation techniques (ds p. 23).

## Pitfalls

- NRESET and DIO3 are referred to VBAT, not VBAT_IO (Table 3-10 note 1, ds p. 21). With a 1.8 V VBAT_IO and 3.3 V VBAT, the NRESET low threshold is 0.2 × VBAT.
- VDD_IN goes to VBAT (pin 10) on the SX1262, but to VREG (pin 7) on the SX1261 (ds p. 12). Do not copy an SX1261 schematic.
- +22 dBm needs VBAT ≥ 3.3 V (ds p. 34); below 2.7 V the output degrades, and at 1.8 V it plateaus near +17 dBm (ds p. 30).
- After SetDIO3AsTCXOCtrl, only a full reset returns the chip to crystal operation (ds p. 24).
- The default OCP is 60 mA until SetPaConfig sets 140 mA for the SX1262 (ds p. 32); +22 dBm draws 118 mA (ds p. 17).
- Change crystal trim only in STDBY_XOSC; the state machine overwrites the registers on entry (ds p. 23).
- RF input above 0 dBm is outside the operating range; 10 dBm is the absolute limit (ds p. 14).

## Not established by the sources

The library pad numbers, names and footprint geometry agree with the data sheet (Table 2-1 and Figure 2-1, ds pp. 12–13; Figure 16-3, ds p. 106). Gaps and disagreements:

- **Source URL:** the README gives only `https://www.semtech.com`, Semtech's home page. Neither the README nor the PDF gives a document URL.
- **RF matching:** the data sheet gives no matching network, filter or antenna-switch values for RFO, RFI_P and RFI_N, and no reference design; it only says VR_PA connects to RFO externally (ds p. 26). The library offers no guidance.
- **+22 dBm supply:** Figure 5-3 says VBAT 3.3 V minimum for +22 dBm (ds p. 34); Table 4-4 says VBAT = 3.1 V for +22 dBm (ds p. 31). This note uses the stricter value.
- **47 nH inductor:** p. 32 refers to "the 47 nH inductor before pin 1" in DC-DC mode, but no figure in the data sheet shows where it goes or what it filters.
- **Library comment:** `sx1262.cohdl` says "433/868/915 MHz"; the data sheet's range is continuous 150–960 MHz (ds p. 18).
- **Thermal:** no θJA or other thermal resistance, and no thermal-via count or drill, for the SX1262.
- **Tmr:** Table 3-2 labels −55 to 125 °C only "Temperature" (ds p. 14); it does not say storage or junction.
- **Courtyard:** the library's 5.3 mm courtyard is not from the source.
