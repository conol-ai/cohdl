## Summary

2450AT18B100 is a 3.2 × 1.6 × 1.3 mm ceramic chip antenna for 2400–2500 MHz with a 50 Ω feed (ds p. 1). It has two terminals: 1 = FEED and 2 = NC (ds p. 1). On Johanson's evaluation board it gives 0.5 dBi peak gain, −0.5 dBi average gain and 76 % radiation efficiency, at 9.5 dB minimum return loss (ds p. 1). Those figures assume the evaluation board's layout: the antenna sits at a board edge in a 6.5 × 6.5 mm area with no ground, behind a three-part matching network (ds p. 2). Any other board needs its own matching. The E suffix is tape and reel, 100 % tin (ds p. 1).

## Typical application

From the evaluation board, Mounting Considerations 1 (ds p. 2):
- 50 Ω feed line (width designed for 50 Ω on the board stack-up) → 1.2 pF (Johanson 500R07S1R2BV4T) in series → 2.7 nH (L-07C2N7SV6T) from that node to ground → 3.3 nH (L-07C3N3SV6T) in series → antenna terminal 1. The topology is read from the drawing; the text lists only the part values.
- These values are for Johanson's board; a different board, enclosure or nearby copper needs re-tuning (README; the source offers tuning services, ds p. 3, p. 5).

## Layout notes

Evaluation-board layout (ds p. 2, units mm):
- Board 39.5 + 6.5 mm long and 13.5 + 6.5 mm wide, with ground everywhere except the antenna corner.
- The antenna sits in a 6.5 × 6.5 mm no-ground area at the board corner/edge; a 4 mm dimension runs from the no-ground area's inner edge toward the antenna.
- Antenna lands: 1.6 mm tall, 2.6 mm between inner edges, 4.2 mm across outer edges, so each land is 0.8 mm wide (computed).

Library `FP_ANT_2450AT18B100E`: two 0.8 × 1.6 mm lands at x = ±1.7 mm, i.e. 2.6 mm inner gap and 4.2 mm outer span. That matches the evaluation-board lands. The footprint does not include the no-ground area, the board-edge placement or the matching network.

## Pitfalls

- Keep ground out of the 6.5 × 6.5 mm "NO GND" region around the antenna (ds p. 2). The source does not say which layers that covers.
- Do not connect terminal 2 (NC) to ground or to the feed; on the evaluation board it is an isolated land (ds p. 2).
- Do not expect the published gain and efficiency without the board-edge placement and the matching network (ds p. 1, p. 2).
- Pin 1 marking on the body is not defined by the source; orient the part by the reel, not by guessing from the mark.
