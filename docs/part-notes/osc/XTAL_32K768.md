## Summary

ABS07 is Abracon's seam-sealed 32.768 kHz tuning-fork crystal, 3.2 × 1.5 × 0.9 mm (ds p. 1). Decoded from the p. 3 option table, the library part is the standard-height, 9 pF load, -40 °C to +85 °C, ±20 ppm configuration on 3k-piece tape and reel (ds p. 3). In this range ESR is at most 70 kΩ and drive level at most 0.5 µW (ds p. 1). Use it as an RTC or low-power clock crystal. Do not use it where ppm accuracy over temperature matters without compensation: the parabolic temperature coefficient (-0.036 ppm/°C² typical, ds p. 1) moves the frequency by tens of ppm at the ends of the range.

## Typical application

- Pierce oscillator: one terminal to each oscillator pin, each with a load capacitor to ground. CL is 9 pF (ds p. 3). The source gives no external capacitor values or stray capacitance.
- Keep the drive level at or below 0.5 µW (ds p. 1). Many 32 kHz oscillators have a drive-strength setting; use the lowest one that starts reliably.
- Frequency error versus temperature follows the parabola on ds p. 2 (turn-over 25 °C ± 5 °C, -0.036 ppm/°C² typical, ds p. 1).

## Layout notes

- Recommended land (ds p. 4): two 1.1 × 1.9 mm lands with a 1.4 mm inner gap. `FP_Crystal_SMD_3215_2Pin_ABS07` matches: pads at x = ±1.25 mm. The 1.4 mm dimension is drawn between the inner land edges with outside arrows.
- Body 3.2 ± 0.1 × 1.50 ± 0.1 mm. Terminals are 0.6 mm wide and 1.3 ± 0.1 mm tall, with a 1.8 mm gap between them (ds p. 4).
- MSL not applicable (ds p. 1). Seam sealed (ds p. 4).
- The source gives no guidance on guard rings, trace length or keep-outs.

## Pitfalls

- The default ABS07 load capacitance is 12.5 pF; this part is the 9 pF option (ds p. 1, p. 3). Size the load capacitors for 9 pF, not 12.5 pF.
- ESR rises to 95 kΩ max for the extended temperature options H and W (ds p. 1). This part is the standard -40 °C to +85 °C option, with 70 kΩ max.
- Drive level above 0.5 µW is outside the specification (ds p. 1). Tuning-fork crystals are easily overdriven.
