## Summary

43045-0412 is the 4-circuit, finish-A (reflowed matte tin) part of Molex's Micro-Fit (3.0) vertical through-hole header family (sd p. 1, finish table). The housing is glass-filled LCP, UL94V-0, black, with brass terminals (sd p. 1, note 1). It mates with Micro-Fit (3.0) receptacle series 43025 (sd p. 1, note 5). Its 4 circuits sit in two rows of 2 on a 3.00 mm grid, with two PCB polarization pegs (sd p. 1). **The sales drawing gives no current or voltage rating.** It refers to product specification PS-43045 (sd p. 1, note 3), which is not in the repository.

## Layout notes

- The PCB layout is the component-side view (sd p. 1, "PCB LAYOUT: COMPONENT SIDE"). Holes are Ø1.02 ± 0.05 mm, the same for contact and peg holes (TYP). Rows are 3.00 ± 0.10 mm apart. The peg holes sit 0.94 ± 0.05 mm beyond the row that holds the last circuit, C = 9.00 mm apart and centred on the contact columns (sd p. 1).
- In that view, circuit 1 is at the lower right and circuit 2 to its left in the same row. The last circuit is at the upper left, in the row next to the pegs. Each row is numbered right to left, so circuit n + N/2 sits directly above circuit n (sd p. 1, PCB layout; the top view on the same page agrees).
- **Library check, done by the agent.** `FP_Molex_43045_0412` has pads 1 (0, 0), 2 (3, 0), 3 (0, 3) and 4 (3, 3), and NPTH Ø1.02 at (−3, 3.94) and (6, 3.94), in CoHDL's +y-down top-view frame. Rotating the component-side layout 180° gives exactly this. The pegs come out at the column centre ±C/2 = 1.5 ± 4.5 mm, and 3.00 + 0.94 = 3.94 mm. No reflection is involved (sd p. 1, PCB layout figure).
- The library's 1.50 mm copper ring is not on the drawing. The drawing gives only hole sizes (sd p. 1).
- Molex may ship the part with only one of the two polarization pegs (sd p. 1, note by the 3-D view). Drill both holes.

## Pitfalls

- Molex numbers row-major, two rows of N/2 (1, 2 / 3, 4), not odd/even across the rows like a generic 2 × N header (sd p. 1). Do not substitute a generic 2 × 2 symbol or land.
- Do not take a current rating from catalogues or memory into a design that cites this note. The pinned source has none (sd p. 1, note 3).

## Not established by the sources

- Current rating, voltage rating, temperature range, mating cycles and derating. All of these are in PS-43045, which is not vendored. The figures in `lib/connectors/docs/README.md` ("8.5 A", "600 V", "−40 to +105 °C", "30 cycles") are not backed by a pinned source.
- Copper annulus. The library uses 1.50 mm; the drawing gives none.
- Whether the peg holes should be plated. The library uses NPTH; the drawing does not say.
- How the housing outline sits relative to the holes in plan view. The agent could not dimension it from the drawing, so the library courtyard's y extent (−2.97 to +6.81 mm) was not checked.
- Revision date. `lib/connectors/docs/README.md` gives "Rev G1, 2021-01-11", but that date does not appear on the drawing. The title block shows G1 with EC UCP2016-4586 dated 2015/12/10 and approval on 2016/08/23 (sd p. 1).
